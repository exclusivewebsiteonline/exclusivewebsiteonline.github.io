Our story

The story behind Morbid Merry-Go-Round

01 How it started

Spooky, sweet and painted by hand

Jenny Fontana paints dark, spooky art: ghostly animals, haunted cemeteries and little lost souls, rendered in soft pastels.

Every print in the shop is a print of one of her own paintings, like the frolicking graveyard lamb from “Someone is Frolicking Over My Grave.”

You can also find her original work in gallery shows and at her booth around Portland. And no AI, ever.

— Jenny

02 Where & how it’s made

Painted first, then printed

Each design begins as an original painting by Jenny. Fine art giclee prints are made with her printing partner, then sealed in a photo bag with a sturdy backing and shipped from Oregon.

  1. Painted. Original paintings by Jenny.
  2. Printed. Fine art giclee prints.
  3. Packed. Sealed and shipped with care.
