Our story

The story behind Little Tail Ceramics

01 How it started

Whimsical creatures with a wise knowing-ness

My inspirations come from natural design, industrial and mid century patterns plus a rustic kind of gypsy folk art and the universal need for connection. The creatures are whimsical, yet there is a wise knowing-ness about them and a trickster attitude.

All of my work is original and hand built. I do not slip cast. Everything I do has layers of color.

My studio muses are my cats. Martin, the incredible fetch cat, and Bethesda, the best little pistol that ever was, still inspire my work, and Louis, with his huge eyes and adorable snark, influences my kitty sculptures.

— Elizabeth

02 Where & how it’s made

Hand built and fired in her home studio

Elizabeth fires her ceramics onsite, to cone 6, in her electric kiln. Firing to cone 6 makes her drawer pulls more durable than similar ceramic pulls from big retailers, and the backs are attached with 2 part epoxy.

  1. Built. Every piece is hand built, never slip cast.
  2. Fired. Fired twice to cone 6, with layers of glaze in between.
  3. Finished. Drawer pulls get sturdy backs set with 2 part epoxy.
