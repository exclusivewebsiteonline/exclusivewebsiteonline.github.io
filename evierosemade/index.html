Our story

The story behind Evierosemade

01 How it started

Craft, design and a love of textiles

With a BA Hons degree in Craft and Design, training in millinery techniques and a huge passion for textiles, I make headwear that is not only a delight to look at but above all well made.

I aim for features such as hidden stitching and a minimalistic modern look which isn’t complicated or over designed. I hand cover all of my headbands with luxury fabrics and hand make many of my statement flowers, corsages and embellishments.

I don’t use synthetic velveteen or satin sponge headbands. I love using silk and luxury textiles, which keep your hair looking great and feeling hydrated during your event.

— Caroline

02 Where & how it’s made
