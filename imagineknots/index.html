Our story

The story behind Imagine Knots

01 How it started

It all started with a family gift

Imagine Knots began after a sister-in-law made amigurumi for the kids, and the idea of making little crochet friends stuck.

The first crochet plants were sold at a small market to raise funds for a charity, and the kids encouraged their mom to keep going.

Today you can find Imagine Knots at markets around Vancouver, like the Vancouver Etsy Co pop up at Robson Square, and at Aloe Beara on West 10th Avenue.

— Imagine Knots

02 Where & how it’s made

Crocheted stitch by stitch

Every flower pot, plant and plushie is crocheted by hand, with a little smiling face, and many can be personalized with an expression and a punny gift tag.

  1. Yarn. Soft yarn in bright, happy colors.
  2. Crocheted. Every petal and pot made by hand.
  3. Smiles. Finished with a cheerful little face.
