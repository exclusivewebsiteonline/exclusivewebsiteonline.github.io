Our story

The story behind MBE Jewellery

01 How it started

A jewellery lover who got hooked

Estelle has always loved jewellery — and admits to owning far too much of it. When the chance came to take a jewellery-making course, she signed up to learn how to make even more, and she was hooked.

MBE stands for “Made by Estelle”. Since 2020 she has been making fun, cute, artisan pieces in sterling silver, copper and glass from Birmingham — from music-note earrings and toy-brick sets to delicate flowers, hearts, pomegranates and mezuzahs.

You can also find her in person at Birmingham Open Studios and local Christmas fairs.

— Estelle

02 Where & how it’s made

Handmade & hallmarked in Birmingham

MBE Jewellery has its own UK Assay Office maker’s hallmark.

  1. Designed. Playful ideas, from question marks to swallows, sketched into wearable pieces.
  2. Handcrafted. Each piece is cut, shaped, textured and finished by hand in sterling silver, copper or brass.
  3. Hallmarked. Many silver pieces carry Estelle’s MBE maker’s mark, verified by the Birmingham Assay Office.
