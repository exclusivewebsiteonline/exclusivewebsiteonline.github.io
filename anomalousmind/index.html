Our story

The story behind Anomalous Mind

01 How it started

Making it unique, wonderful and all my own

I have associate degrees in Fine Art and Small Business Management. My dream is to one day open a community-oriented art studio and gallery that offers classes and a place for creative people to gather.

As an artist, I love to experiment: new media, new techniques, new styles. I start with an established technique, then alter it and change things up until I’ve transformed it into something unique.

My inspiration comes a lot from nature, both in design and in the idea of recycling: taking something that has run its course and turning it into something brand new. Like in nature, I try to make many of my pieces functional.

— Caroline

02 Where & how it’s made

Sewn to be useful and beautiful

Every bag is cut and sewn by Caroline with sturdy zippers, fun fabrics and sizes made for real knitting and crochet projects.

  1. Chosen. Fun fabric prints, picked fresh.
  2. Sewn. Zippered bags and pouches made by hand.
  3. Used. Sized for 1-2 skein to sweater projects.

What we make

Project bags and fiber-art goods

Knitting project bags, notion pouches, matching sets and fabric. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Knitting project bags

    Zippered project bags in 1-2 skein and sweater sizes, with a handy finger loop.

  • Notion pouches

    Flat and stand-up pouches for stitch markers, needles and little tools.

  • Matching sets

    Project bags and pouches in the same print, made to go together.

    Batik fabric, knit-stitch graph paper and other handmade wonders.
