Our story

The story behind Doggy Dressing Room

01 How it started

Made for Yasmin, shared with you

When I went looking for bows for my Shih Tzu Yasmin, so many were plain, or damaged her hair. I knew I could do better for her.

So I started making them myself. What began as a hobby turned into a creative business that brings together my two loves: making things and animals.

Today I make bows, bandanas, birthday hats and bow ties for pups everywhere, plus a 12 Days of Hair Bows advent calendar for the holidays.

— Yamirelys

02 Where & how it’s made

Handmade, gentle on hair, full of fun

Every bow and bandana is made by hand in Massachusetts, designed to look adorable and stay kind to your pup’s coat.

  1. Designed. Seasonal colors, ribbons and pom poms.
  2. Handmade. Each bow made by Yamirelys.
  3. Pup approved. Gentle on hair, tested by Yasmin.
