Our story

The story behind Stoned and Fired Pottery

01 How it started

Welcome to our family-owned pottery studio.

Every piece is thoughtfully handcrafted, from wheel-thrown mugs and bowls to hand-painted travel sippers, trays, spoon rests and one-of-a-kind functional art.

Our work is inspired by imagination, humor, nature and a love of storytelling. Whether you’re looking for something cute and whimsical, delightfully quirky or wonderfully spooky, you’ll find a little bit of everything.

You’ll also find our pottery locally at Black Cat Coffee Roasters and DaVinci Tattoo Studio, and at art shows, festivals and vendor events around the Space Coast.

— Daniel & Amanda

02 Where & how it’s made

Porcelain, solar power and a lot of imagination

We primarily work with silky-smooth B-Mix porcelain clay, with the occasional adventure into speckled stoneware, so every piece is as practical as it is beautiful.

  1. Thrown & cast. Hand-thrown on the wheel or slipcast in the studio.
  2. Hand-painted. Every design is painted and glazed by hand.
  3. Solar-powered. Our studio is proudly solar-powered.

What we make

Pottery that brings a smile

Travel sippers, wheel-thrown mugs, quirky and spooky designs, teapots, trays and more. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Travel sippers

    Hand-painted 15 oz ceramic travel sippers: fairy bottles, octopus tentacles, lavender, clowns and cats.

    Big 20 oz wheel-thrown stoneware mugs, from anatomical designs to dancing ghosts.

  • Cute, quirky & spooky
