Our story

The story behind Nimble Sew & Stitch

01 How it started

From hand embroidery to patterns for everyone.

I have recently re-branded from ‘Nimble Hand Embroidery’ to Nimble Sew and Stitch. While I loved making beautiful hand-embroidered items for customers, unfortunately the process is just too slow to earn enough money.

So I have begun to create digital sewing and embroidery patterns that help others learn to create their own handmade products at home.

I also create handmade artworks inspired by various landscapes of places I have visited. I hope I can inspire you to learn to sew or embroider too :)

— Sarah

02 Where & how it’s made

Patterns made for beginners

All patterns are beginner-friendly and come with video tutorials and demonstrations.

  1. Download. Every pattern is a printable PDF download.
  2. Watch. Follow the video tutorials and demonstrations, step by step.
  3. Stitch. Make your own handmade pieces at home, from coasters to floral borders.
