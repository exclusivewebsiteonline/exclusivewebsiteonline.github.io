Our story

The story behind Serendipity Draws

01 How it started

Studio art, turned into a shop

I studied Studio Art & Design at the University of Idaho, and I’ve been sharing my art through my shop since 2018.

These days the shop is full of stickers, enamel pins, holographic keychains and standees, plus plushies that came to life through a Kickstarter campaign.

— Rae

02 What’s in the shop

Art for the games and shows you love

A few of the worlds my art visits:

  1. Genshin Impact. Fuzzy Friends plushies and Seelie pet enamel pins.
  2. Honkai: Star Rail. Holographic keychains, stickers and standees.
  3. Stardew Valley. Cute stickers, like the Krobus roommates one.
  4. Arcane. Season 2 holographic keychains.
