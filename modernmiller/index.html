Our story

The story behind Modern Miller

01 How it started

Born on maternity leave.

While I was home on maternity leave and stuck in the house, I was craving a place to channel my creative energy.

Modern Miller was born from the desire to create custom and one-of-a-kind goods for you and your family.

— Allie

02 Where & how it’s made

Custom embroidery for your littles

Every piece is made to order with your little one’s name.

  1. Pick the piece. Sweatshirts, matching sets, knit rompers, hats, blankets or a hooped name sign.
  2. Add the name. Each one is embroidered with the name you choose.
  3. Made for you. Custom and one of a kind, made for your family.
