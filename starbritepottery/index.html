Our story

The story behind Starbrite Pottery

01 How it started

Steeped in nature, shaped by hand

Making pottery is a culmination of years of using tools and decorative techniques in woodworking, folk art painting, crafting, and making in many mediums for over 30 years. It is the most fulfilling of all of my endeavors.

I lived on my parents' farm in Sparta, NJ, as a young girl, and later on family farms in Arkansas and at the organic Starbrite Farm in Hardwick, NJ. These farms steeped my soul in deep connections to nature, which easily shows in all that I create.

Beach themes emerge from fond memories of Maine and other seashore trips. Marrying my love of decorating, carving, hand engraving and building helps bring my pottery more to life for you to enjoy.

— Teresa

02 Where & how it’s made

Wheel thrown, carved and glazed

Teresa throws and alters stoneware, then carves, engraves and paints it before glazing it in earthy, textured glazes.

  1. Thrown. Stoneware shaped on the wheel and altered by hand.
  2. Decorated. Carved, engraved and painted with vines and leaves.
  3. Glazed. Finished in earthy, drippy and textured glazes.
