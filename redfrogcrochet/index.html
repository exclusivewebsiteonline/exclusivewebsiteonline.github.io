Our story

The story behind Red Frog Crochet

01 How it started

A yarn addict and her one of a kind makes

Crochet and knit, yarn addict, handmade custom gifts: that is Red Frog Crochet in a nutshell.

Whether a piece starts from a designer's pattern or from no pattern at all, when you purchase from RFC your item will be one of a kind in some way or another, from the colors to the size.

Thank you for visiting my small business page for RFC!

— Debra

02 Where & how it’s made

Made stitch by stitch

Every beanie, ear warmer and cozy is crocheted or knit by hand by Debra, often in luxurious Malabrigo Rasta merino wool, and custom orders are always welcome.

  1. Yarn. Soft, chunky yarns like Malabrigo Rasta merino wool.
  2. Stitched. Crocheted or knit by hand, one at a time.
  3. One of a kind. Colors and sizes make every piece unique.
