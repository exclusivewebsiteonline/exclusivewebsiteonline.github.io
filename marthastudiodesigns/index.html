Our story

The story behind Martha Studio Designs

01 How it started

Where every piece tells a story.

From my cozy studio in Houston, Texas, I create handmade jewelry and fashion pens that are not only beautiful but deeply personal.

I started with a simple goal: to design affordable, custom-made pieces that speak to the soul. No two are ever exactly the same, because every person is unique.

As a proud autism mom, wife and business owner, I pour strength, love and resilience into everything I create. When you order, you’re supporting a dream, a family and a labor of love.

— Martha

02 Where & how it’s made

Made by hand, one bead at a time

Each design is lovingly made by hand using high-quality materials, and beautifully packaged for gifting.

  1. Designed. Colors chosen to tell a story.
  2. Beaded. Czech glass seed beads, woven by hand.
  3. Packaged. Gift-ready presentation, packed with care.
