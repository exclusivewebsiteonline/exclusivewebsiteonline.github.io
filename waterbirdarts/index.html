Our story

The story behind Water Bird Arts

01 How it started

Inspired by a love of materials

Inspired by a love of materials, handcrafted objects, culture, and nature; at the intersection of these is usually where you'll find me. I have a BFA in Sculpture and love the concept of portable, wearable art. I also enjoy drawing, painting, book illustration, glass, beadwork, basketry and sculpture.

Water Bird Arts began in November 2009 as Roots and Wings Designs. After a hiatus I reopened in 2019, focusing on small metals, beadwork and prints of pen and ink drawings. The first book I illustrated, The Cherokee Syllabary, was released in 2024, followed by the Baby's First Cherokee books.

I am from Dallas, TX, an enrolled Cherokee Nation citizen, TERO certified with Cherokee Nation, registered with the Indian Arts and Crafts Board, and a board member of the Southeastern Indian Artists Association.

— Beth

02 Where & how it’s made

Hand fabricated from natural materials

Beth works with sheet and wire metal and natural gemstones, hand stamping and fabricating each piece. Her illustrations become prints, stickers, journals and books.

  1. Designed. Rooted in Cherokee culture and the natural world.
  2. Fabricated. Sterling silver and brass, hand stamped and formed.
  3. Shared. Packed with care and a personal note.
