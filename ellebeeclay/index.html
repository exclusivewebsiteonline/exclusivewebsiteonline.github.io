Our story

The story behind Elle Bee Clay

01 How it started

I fell in love with polymer clay.

I have always had a passion for art and creativity, and I have fallen in love with polymer clay.

Elle Bee Clay specializes in unique and colorful polymer clay earrings: stripes, rainbows, marbled prints and bright solids, all lightweight and made by hand.

It brings me so much joy to share my creations with you, and I hope you love them as much as I do! Find me on Instagram for a fun look behind the scenes and sneak peeks of what’s coming soon.

— Laura Becca

02 Where & how it’s made

Made by hand, made to be worn

Every pair is designed and made by hand from polymer clay, so it’s light enough to wear all day.

  1. Colors & patterns. Stripes, rainbows, marbled and mixed prints in bright, happy colors.
  2. Shaped by hand. Arches, circles, ovals and squares, cut and finished by hand.
  3. Light to wear. Lightweight earrings, comfortable for sensitive ears.

What we make

Colorful earrings for every day

Statement dangles, stripes and mixed prints, basics, studs and seasonal styles. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Statement dangles

    Big, bright rainbow and marbled statement earrings and shoulder dusters that are still lightweight.

  • Stripes & mixed prints

    Colorful stripes, rainbow prints and fun mixed patterns in circles, ovals and arches.

  • Basics

    Simple solid and textured dangles in lots of colors, comfortable for sensitive ears.

    Colorful studs, plus Halloween and fall designs when the seasons change.
