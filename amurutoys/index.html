Our story

The story behind Amuru Toys

01 How it started

Little animals in cozy sweaters.

Every Amuru Toys friend is a unique handmade stuffed animal: foxes, squirrels, deer, badgers, bears, lions, bunnies and cats.

Each one is hand-stitched from felt and dressed in its own cozy sweater, vest or scarf.

If you love to sew, my easy sewing patterns come with instructions and photos, so you can make your own.

— Anna

02 Where & how it’s made

Hand-stitched, one at a time

Every toy is cut, stitched and dressed by hand, and ships worldwide.

  1. Cut. Soft felt, cut for each animal.
  2. Stitched. Hand-stitched faces, ears and tails.
  3. Dressed. Cozy sweaters, scarves and vests.
