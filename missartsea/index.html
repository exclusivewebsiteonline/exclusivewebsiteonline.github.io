Our story

The story behind Miss Artsea

01 How it started

A natural calling to calligraphy and watercolour

Welcome to Miss Artsea, a shop where I combine all that I love into one place.

Ever since a young age, I have always loved expressing myself creatively. After experimenting with all types of mediums, I felt a natural calling to calligraphy and watercolour. With my art, I aspire to highlight the elegance & beauty of nature’s simplicity.

Whether you’re looking for something beautiful for yourself or a personalized gift for someone special, I’d love to create something just for you.

— Joey

02 Where & how it’s made

Lovingly hand-painted, one at a time

Every piece is lovingly hand-painted and handmade, making each one truly unique and one of a kind. Have a custom idea? Send a message and let’s bring your vision to life.

  1. Chat. Share your idea, photo or names.
  2. Painted. Watercolour and calligraphy, all by hand.
  3. Sent. Carefully packaged, ready to gift.
