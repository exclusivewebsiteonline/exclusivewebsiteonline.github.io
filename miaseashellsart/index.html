Our story

My little coastal world

01 How it started

A little piece of the ocean

Mia Sea Shells Art is my little coastal world, where I create one-of-a-kind handmade home décor using real seashells collected and carefully selected from around the world.

Each piece is individually designed, making every creation unique: a little piece of the ocean to enjoy in your home.

— Maiia

02 Where & how it’s made

Arranged by hand, shell by shell

I create each piece by hand, carefully arranging shells to highlight their unique shapes, textures and delicate shimmer.

  1. Collected. Real seashells, collected and carefully selected from around the world.
  2. Arranged by hand. Each shell is placed to show off its shape, texture and shimmer.
  3. One of a kind. Natural details give every creation its own character.
