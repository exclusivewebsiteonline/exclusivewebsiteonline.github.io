Meet the Artist

Hi, I’m Molly.

Owner & artist of PoppyTales Farm

My name is Molly O’Sullivan and I am the owner of PoppyTales Farm in Perkiomenville, Pennsylvania. I have a small herd of dairy goats on my farm that I milk to make my homemade goat milk soap and goat milk lotion.

Our story

The story behind PoppyTales Farm

01 How it started

Made from scratch on the farm

Along with the goats, the farm is also home to about 50 chickens and a handful of quail. Soon we will be expanding to add cows to our farm too!

I sell my soap to help support the farm. All profit made from selling my soap and lotion goes right back into the farm for buying feed and other equipment for the animals, as well as vet bills.

You can follow along with the farming adventures on Instagram and Facebook @poppytalesfarm and on TikTok @poppytales.

— Molly

02 Where & how it’s made

Small batches, raw goat milk

Each bar of soap and bottle of lotion is made in a small batch from scratch, using high quality ingredients and my own raw goat milk from the farm. I take great pride in the quality of the products that I make.

  1. Milked. Fresh raw milk from our own dairy goats.
  2. Made. Small batches, from scratch, on the farm.
  3. Cured & wrapped. Carefully wrapped and packaged for you.
