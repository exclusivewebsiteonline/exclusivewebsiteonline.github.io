Our story

The story behind Four Little Buttons

01 How it started

Soft, sweet and sewn in NSW

Every romper, pair of harem pants and knot hat is sewn by hand in New South Wales from certified organic cotton.

The prints are full of personality: kangaroos for an Australian summer, gnomes and candy canes for a first Christmas, boho florals and gingham.

Parents around the world say the same thing: the fabric is so soft, the work is beautifully made, and the outfits arrive fast.

— Four Little Buttons

02 Where & how it’s made

Certified organic, sewn by hand

Penelope cuts and sews each piece in small drops, choosing soft certified organic cotton knits that are kind to brand new skin.

  1. Organic. Certified organic cotton fabrics.
  2. Sewn. Each piece made by hand in NSW.
  3. Gifted. Ready for baby, or a baby shower.
