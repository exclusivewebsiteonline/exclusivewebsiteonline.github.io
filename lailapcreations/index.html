Our story

The story behind Laila P. Creations

01 How it started

An outlet for my sewing and creativity.

I started my shop as an outlet for my sewing and creativity. I love taking something as simple as fabric and creating an heirloom quality keepsake.

Combining colors is my strength, and I love the planning process and choosing the fabrics for each project. I have enjoyed creating t-shirt quilts as well.

See what I am working on for the shop, family and friends on YouTube and Instagram. Look around, and be blessed!

— Corynne

02 Where & how it’s made

Sewn by hand, made for stitchers

Project bags hold all your cross stitch, needlework and craft storage needs. I would love to create a custom quilt for you too.

  1. Choose the fabric. Only high quality fabrics, with colors combined with care.
  2. Sew & finish. Every bag, guard and quilt is sewn by me.
  3. Ready to stitch. Roomy enough for bigger projects, and packed with a little treat.

What we make

For your stitching, sewing and home

Project bags, seasonal and themed bags, grime guards, modern quilts and custom orders. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Project bags

    Roomy zippered project bags for cross stitch, needlework and craft storage, in high quality fabrics.

  • Seasonal & themed bags

    Holiday, sports team and fun print project bags, a new favorite for every season.

  • Grime guards

    Fabric grime guards to protect your work on Q-snap frames and Nurge hoops.

  • Quilts & custom orders

    Modern quilts, t-shirt quilts and custom quilts. Just send a message, and layaway is available.
