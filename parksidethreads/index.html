Our story

The story behind Parkside Threads

01 How it started

Quilts to treasure and pass down

My first love is creating heirloom quilts for you to treasure, use in your home and pass down to future generations. They’re made to be used on beds, sofas, picnics and at the beach, and make lovely gifts for weddings, new babies, graduations and new homes.

I live in the beautiful Finger Lakes region of western New York with my husband, our two daughters and Henry, our big Golden Retriever. We walk or hike with him every day!

I’ve been sewing since I was a little girl, started quilting in 2019 and opened my shop in February 2022.

— Susan

02 Where & how it’s made

Nothing goes to waste

My totes, zippered pouches, coasters and key chains are sometimes made with leftover quilt fabric. I like to use what I have and not let fabric go to waste.

  1. Chosen. Only the highest quality fabrics and supplies.
  2. Sewn. Pieced, quilted and finished in my home studio.
  3. Loved. Made to be used every day and passed down.

What we make

Fabric creations for your home

Quilts, bags, pouches and little gifts for kids. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Heirloom quilts

    Baby, toddler and throw quilts made to be used, loved and passed down.

  • Totes & crossbody bags

    Slim crossbody fanny packs, market totes and canvas totes in pretty fabrics.

  • Pouches & toiletry bags

    Quilted makeup bags, zipper pouches and water-resistant toiletry bags.

  • Play crowns & little gifts

    Reversible pretend-play crowns, stuffed bunnies in leaf pockets and stockings.
