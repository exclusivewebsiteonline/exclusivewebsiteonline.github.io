Our story

The story behind American Art & Glass

01 How it started

American made, from Michigan

American Art & Glass makes hand sculpted and blown glass miniatures, bottles and more, right here in Michigan.

We take pride in using American materials and creating American made products. The shop also uses Michigan Made boxes and packaging, and recycles whenever possible.

Check back regularly for new and unique pieces, from tiny pumpkins and potion bottles to pendants and planters. We also welcome your ideas!

— Amy

02 Where & how it’s made

Sculpted and blown by hand

Each piece is shaped by hand in the flame, so no two are ever exactly alike. Orders are packed carefully so they arrive safe and sound.

  1. Shaped. Glass is hand sculpted and blown in the flame.
  2. Finished. Each piece is one of a kind, in bright colours.
  3. Packed. Securely wrapped in Michigan Made boxes.

What we make

Hand sculpted glass for all ages

Glass miniatures, pendants, blown glass bottles, stirrers and ornaments. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Glass miniatures

    Tiny pumpkins, turtles, ghosts and mushroom gardens, hand sculpted one at a time.

  • Glass pendants

    Daisies, hearts, paw prints and little animals to wear every day.

  • Blown glass bottles

    Little potion bottles and stash jars with corks and charms.

  • Stirrers & ornaments

    Heart cocktail stirrers, swizzle sticks, icicles and Michigan mitten ornaments.
