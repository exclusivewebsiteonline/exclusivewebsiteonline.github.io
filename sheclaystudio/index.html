02 Where & how it’s made

Made in small batches

“If the shop is empty, I’m working on my next batch.”

  1. Thrown by hand. Every mug is thrown, trimmed and finished by me.
  2. Painted and glazed. Speckled, drippy and rustic glazes with hand-painted designs.
  3. Restocked in batches. New batches go live on restock days, announced on Instagram.
