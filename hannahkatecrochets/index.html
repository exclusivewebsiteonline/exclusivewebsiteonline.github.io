Our story

The story behind Hannah Kate Crochets

01 How it started

I learned to crochet when I was 12.

I learned to crochet from a neighbor when I was 12. I started again when I got married, 11 years ago, and I’ve been honing the skill ever since.

I didn’t know you could create animals with crochet until I saw an adorable turtle on Pinterest, so I made my first amigurumi for a dear friend’s first baby.

In 2022 I started Hannah Kate, Tx, and in 2024 I connected deeply with the crochet community and narrowed my business down to crochet. Now I work to curate a crochet line for everyone in the family.

— Hannah
