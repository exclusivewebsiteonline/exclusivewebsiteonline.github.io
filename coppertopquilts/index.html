Our story

The story behind Copper Top Quilts

01 How it started

Quilts made to be loved and used.

Copper Top Quilts showcases quilts made by me, Kristina K. Morris. Some are for sale, and commissions are accepted.

I love my community of quilters. Give me Dateline, quilting, art, Broadway and museums!

My quilts are made to be used: one of them was recently spotted in the front row at Cardinal Music Hall. The shop has just been refreshed with all new items, so take a look.

— Kristina

02 Where & how it’s made

Pieced & quilted, one quilt at a time

From small wall hangings to queen-size bed quilts. Have something special in mind? Commissions are accepted.

  1. Designed. Bold, graphic designs: snowflakes, arrows, mountains, peace signs and rainbow instruments.
  2. Under the needle. Every quilt is pieced and quilted by Kristina.
  3. Ready to hang or use. Wall hangings for your home, and quilts for your bed.

What we make

Quilts for your walls and your bed

Quilted wall hangings, modern bed quilts, music-themed quilts and tribute quilts. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Quilted wall hangings

    Bold, graphic wall hangings, from snowflakes and arrows to I LOVE VA and Peace Be With You.

  • Bed & modern quilts

    Full-size modern quilts, like the green ombré Springtime Mountains and the queen-size Shoes, Shoes, Shoes.

  • Rainbow music quilts

    Rainbow banjo and stringed guitar wall hangings for the music lovers.

  • Places & tributes

    Quilts that celebrate places and people, like the Grand Canyon and a Housetops quilt honoring Lillie Pettway of Gee’s Bend.
