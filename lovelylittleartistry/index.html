Our story

The story behind Lovely Little Artistry

01 How it started

Little friends, crocheted by hand

Lovely Little Artistry is Ashley’s little shop of handmade crochet. Every plushie is made to order, usually in about 3 to 7 days.

You’ll find her at craft fairs and markets around Long Island all fall, and sharing daily updates on Instagram of what she’s working on.

She also writes her own crochet patterns, so you can make her cows, ghosts and pirates yourself. Customers say they’re easy to follow and work up fast.

— Ashley

02 Where & how it’s made

Made to order, stitch by stitch

Every plushie is crocheted by hand once you order it, and custom orders are welcome.

  1. Designed. Original characters dreamed up by Ashley.
  2. Crocheted. Made by hand, stitch by stitch.
  3. Shared. As plushies, or as patterns you can make too.
