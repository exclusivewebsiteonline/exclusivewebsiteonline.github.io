Our story

The story behind Acorn Woods Illustrations

01 How it started

Inspired by wildlife and nature

Wildlife and nature inspire everything I paint. Each illustration is meant to be a little glimpse into a world of animals and other gentle beings.

You might find a bear in a flower crown sipping tea, a goblin curled in a teacup, or a ghost and a cat wandering a moonlit wood.

Some pieces start as woodburnings and get painted, others are gouache on paper. Every order is packed with care, often with an extra sticker tucked in.

— Brittni

02 Where & how it’s made

Painted, burned and printed with care

Brittni creates each original by hand, then turns her favorites into art prints and stickers so they can find homes everywhere.

  1. Imagined. Creatures and forests drawn from nature.
  2. Painted. Gouache, paint and woodburning by hand.
  3. Printed. Prints and stickers packed with love.
