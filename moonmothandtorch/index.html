Our story

The story behind Moon, Moth and Torch

01 How it started

Picking the burner back up after a tornado.

I started wood burning in 2021, right after a tornado took nearly everything my fiancé and I had. Picking the burner back up got me through the anxiety of starting over, and I haven’t put it down since.

I’m a hedge witch, a night owl, a horror lover, and nature lover. It all shows up in the work.

— Melissa

02 Where & how it’s made

Burned by hand in a Tennessee home studio

“Every piece in this shop is burned by hand — no lasers, just me, the wood, and a lot of patience and love!”

  1. Fire and wood. Every design is burned by hand, and some pieces are painted by hand too.
  2. Made at night. Padfoot burns the midnight oil with me every night that I am up wood burning, and Terence makes sure I take little breaks.
  3. Custom pieces. I am also available for custom pieces. Never hesitate to reach out and message me about anything.
