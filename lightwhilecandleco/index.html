Our story

The story behind Light While Candle Co.

01 How it started

A candle a day, on the way back to myself.

I go by the gaming tag “Magnus,” though my real name is Michael. The year 2020 cast me into a deep depression. Like many others, I lost my career, my sense of self and the ability to enjoy my favourite hobbies. Hosting was my passion; I even had “the get-together house,” with a pool and a fire pit where friends gathered on warm days.

My therapist suggested I do something creative every day and reconnect with my inner child. I found a listing for old candle-making equipment and 20 pounds of wax for $80. Armed with those rickety tools, Skillshare and YouTube tutorials, I promised myself to create something every single day. That commitment was my lifeline, and it slowly guided me back to a place of mental wellness.

At the same time I found Twitch, Discord and the world of Final Fantasy XIV, and friends from across the U.S., Brazil and the U.K. Then an idea dawned on me: what if I made candles that captured the scents of the gaming worlds I loved? When I named the shop, my partner asked about my goal. I said, “I want to make candles you light while doing something you love.” That’s how “Light While” Candle Company was born.

— Magnus

02 Where & how it’s made

Scents built from the game’s own landscapes

“I delved into essential oils, aiming to mimic the scents of vegetation. I spent hours studying the game’s landscapes, diving deep into its lore, and identifying unique elements in each region.”

  1. Study the world. Each scent starts with a region, a city or a festival from the game and its lore.
  2. Blend the oils. Essential oils are blended to capture that place, from leather-bound books to desert orchid.
  3. Pour by hand. Soy candles and wax melts are hand-crafted in small batches in the U.S.
