Our story

The story behind 3crows

01 How it started

Dreaming beside a vintage amusement park

I dream and create in one of those vintage lakeside amusement parks, where we’ve grown accustomed to the summer sounds of screams as the 100-year-old roller coaster takes its riders on a still thrilling ride. I rode it myself as an early teen, over and over and over.

In my spare time I bird, thrift and walk one of my 3 dogs. The stray we took in turned out to be pregnant, so we kept mom, Mudget, and two of the pups, Franky and Popo (a true wonder at just 3 lbs).

Welcome to my slightly demented animal art world: quirky paintings, prints and pet portraits. I’ve developed numerous series and have a penchant for making things out of found or tossed objects.

— Sara

02 Where & how it’s made

Custom paintings of your families

I love doing custom paintings of your animal and people families, and I find I have something quirky and unique to add to the world of illustration.

  1. Share. Send photos of your pets and people.
  2. Painted. Sara paints them into her own quirky little scene.
  3. Treasured. A one-of-a-kind portrait, or a memorial of a loved one.
