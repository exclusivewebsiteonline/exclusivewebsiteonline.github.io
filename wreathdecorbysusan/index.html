Our story

The story behind Wreath Decor By Susan

01 How it started

How this all began.

I began making wreaths when my daughter was expecting twins, a boy and a girl. I designed a pink and blue mesh wreath for the babies’ shower, and after the compliments I began making more.

Then I made several patriotic wreaths and brought them to a meeting to see if they would sell... and they did! That was the start of Wreath Decor By Susan.

It’s a joy and privilege to design for your home. Items handcrafted by me are designed with love, in the hope that you will treasure them for seasons to come.

— Susan

02 Where & how it’s made

Quality materials in every design

Lush wired ribbons, graceful florals, unique handmade attachments and extra items that coordinate well and add character to every theme.

  1. Designed. Each wreath and centerpiece is designed by Susan.
  2. Layered. Ribbons, florals and greenery, layered by hand.
  3. Finished. Packed with care for your front door or table.
