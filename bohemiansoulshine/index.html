Our story

The story behind Bohemian Soul Shine

01 How it started

Head over heels for a dusty rhinestone

My story is very simple: I love jewelry, therefore I make jewelry. Many years ago I fell head over heels in love with a dusty old rhinestone. I have been smitten ever since.

Bohemian Soul Shine was born out of all things that represent a free spirit, a journey, a story yet to be told. I have a bohemian soul — designing and making jewelry makes my soul shine. I hope you find something here that makes yours shine too.

— Jen

02 Where & how it’s made

Barefoot in the sunshine, then home to the studio

You’ll find me scouring flea markets, antique stores, and estate sales for lovely old forgotten treasures just screaming for new life — then back to my Florida home studio.

  1. Hunt the findings. Vintage rhinestones, spoons, coins, leather, and charms from flea markets and estate sales.
  2. Design by hand. Rustic cuffs, necklaces, and rings with their own story — never mass-produced perfect.
  3. Finish with love. Made in a home studio with sunshine and good vibes — as seen in Belle Armoire.
  4. Ship with care. Each piece leaves ready for the free spirits who wear them.

03 What makes it special

Three things that matter here

Imperfectly perfect

These pieces are rustic, rugged, and worn — with their own story. Please don’t expect mass-produced perfection; you won’t find that here.

Vintage given new life

Findings from the 20s to modern day — rhinestones, spoons, coins, and leather — reworked into jewelry for gypsy-hearted cowgirls.

One woman, sunshiny studio

Made in a Florida home studio with love, sunshine, and good vibes — honored to be featured in Belle Armoire more than once.

What we make

Jewelry for the vintage soul

Bracelets, necklaces, rings, and earrings — rustic handmade pieces from vintage findings, finished in a Florida home studio.

  • Bracelets & cuffs

    Hand-stamped cuffs, spoon bracelets, leather, rhinestone glam, and stainless pieces with a message — the heart of the shop.

  • Necklaces

    Moon and star pendants, coin charms, wings, bees, and layering chains — bold pieces born from history for modern bohemians.

  • Rings

    Celestial moons, dragonflies, and one-of-a-kind bands with that same rustic, soulful feel.

  • Earrings

    Coin fringe, rhinestone drops, and bohemian pairs that catch the light — made to match the free-spirit vibe.
