Our story

The story behind Strawberry Threads

01 How it started

Made for the little ones in your life.

Strawberry Threads is a small hand embroidery shop in New Jersey. Every piece is personalized: a name stitched across a soft knit sweater, a monogram on a kitchen towel, or a sprinkle of flowers on a cardigan.

Many of my pieces are made to be given: for new babies, first birthdays, baby showers, and even a military homecoming.

Choose a sweater color, send me the name, and I’ll stitch it by hand. Custom ideas are always welcome!

— Kay

02 Where & how it’s made

Stitched by hand, one name at a time

Choose from sweater colors like Off the Coast Blue, Cotton Candy, Dusty Blue, Sand, White and Confetti, or ask about a custom design.

  1. Choose a color. Soft knit sweaters and cardigans in infant, toddler and child sizes.
  2. Add the name. A name or initials, plus custom touches like flowers or bees.
  3. Stitched by hand. Each piece is embroidered by hand and shipped to you.
