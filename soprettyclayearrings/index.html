Our story

The story behind So Pretty Clay Earrings

01 How it started

A lifelong creative falls for clay

Then I fell in love with handcrafting earrings with polymer clay. I immersed myself in studying global polymer clay artists’ video tutorials to learn best practices, from basic techniques and clay slabs to advanced canes.

I love creating an eclectic mix of styles in my earring designs, applying both traditional polymer clay methods and modern, trendy fashion clay techniques: flat slabs, layered canes, textured patterns, and mixed media.

I hope that you will cherish each lovely nuance in my handmade polymer clay earrings, and that you’ll love wearing them as much as I enjoy making them in my art studio.

— Pamela

02 Where & how it’s made

From pasta machine to polished edge

I condition high quality polymer clay by hand through my pasta machine, which fully blends colors and makes layers a uniform thickness. Shapes are hand cut and baked, canes are sliced one slice at a time, and every earring is sanded and smoothed.

  1. Conditioned. Clay blended and rolled through the pasta machine.
  2. Cut & baked. Shapes hand cut, then cured for about an hour.
  3. Finished. Sealed, sanded and set on quality brass findings.
