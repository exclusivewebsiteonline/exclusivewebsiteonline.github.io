Our story

The story behind Handmade by Mojo

01 How it started

I love love love the process.

I love love love the process of making earrings and the creativity it inspires in this beautiful world God has given us!

Every pair is made by hand in Westerville, Ohio, from polymer clay and pressed flower resin. You can also find me at local markets and events around Columbus.

I am so incredibly grateful for each person who stops by… you are simply amazing! Thanks and enjoy!

— Jodi

02 Where & how it’s made

Shaped, baked & finished by hand

Want a duplicate of a favorite pair, or something custom? Custom requests are always welcome. Just send me a message!

  1. Designed. Boho, botanical, floral and seasonal styles, plus bridal pairs.
  2. Handmade. Polymer clay shaped and textured by hand, and pressed flowers set in resin.
  3. Ready to wear. Finished with hypoallergenic stainless steel or sterling silver.

What we make

Earrings for every day and every occasion

Dangle earrings, botanical and floral styles, bridal and wedding pairs, and a fall collection. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Boho dangles

    Neutral, geometric and textured polymer clay dangles for everyday wear.

  • Botanical & floral

    Daisies, sunflowers and philodendron leaves, plus pressed flower resin earrings.

  • Bridal & formal

    Elegant bridal dangles and formal earrings for weddings and special days.
