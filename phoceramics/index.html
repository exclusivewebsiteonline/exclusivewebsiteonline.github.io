Our story

Welcome to my magic forest

01 How it started

In love with clay since high school

I started creating back when I was still in middle school, but I only started modelling out of clay during my last years of high school. I fell in love with clay’s plasticity and its properties, and that was the reason I chose to study it further. I applied to Vilnius Academy of Arts, and four years later I was awarded a bachelor’s degree in ceramics.

What I create now did not come quick. It materialized wandering through my childhood’s memories of the forests and fields of my home town, a beautiful place surrounded by woods and huge open fields of grass and rye. Ever since I was little I loved animals, and I still do now.

— Audra

02 Where & how it’s made

Formed by hand in Vilnius

Every Pho animal is formed by hand; only the concept stays constant: a mug or a bowl with a function. Most often a piece takes up to a couple of weeks to finish.

  1. Formed by hand. Each animal is shaped by hand around a useful form.
  2. Eyes painted by brush. Their eyes are a source of life, painted with a small brush using underglaze paint.
  3. Fired and finished. Multiple 24-hour firings in the kiln, glazing, painting, and gold or silver plating.
