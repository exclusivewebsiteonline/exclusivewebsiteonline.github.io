Our story

The story behind Moore Monsters

01 How it started

From masks to monster mugs

I started this shop as a way to get my creations out to the public. I have taken my sculpting and mask making skills and segued into slip cast ceramics.

What makes my work stand out is the subject matter: werewolves, lagoon creatures, aliens, tree demons and classic movie monsters, all sculpted with love for the films and the fans.

I am slowly placing my items in my shop, so bookmark it and see if there's something new.

— Jim

02 Where & how it’s made

Sculpted, cast and hand-finished

Each design starts as an original clay sculpture. Jim brings his sculpting and mask making skills to slip cast ceramics, and hand-finishes every piece.

  1. Sculpted. An original monster sculpted in clay.
  2. Cast. Turned into slip cast ceramic.
  3. Finished. Hand-finished, glazed and packed with care.
