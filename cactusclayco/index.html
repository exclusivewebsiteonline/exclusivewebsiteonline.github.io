Our story

The story behind Cactus Clay Co

01 How it started

From Washington to the Arizona sun

Cactus Clay Co is my little shop of handmade polymer clay earrings, now based out of Surprise, Arizona after a move from Washington.

Every pair is lightweight and handmade with love. I hand mix every color, so slight variations make each pair imperfectly perfect.

You can also find me at local farmers markets, fall festivals and shops around the valley. I’m always creating new things, so check back often!

— Hannah

02 Where & how it’s made

Hand mixed, one pair at a time

Every color is mixed by hand and every pair is shaped, baked and finished in small batches.

  1. Mixed. Every color blended by hand.
  2. Shaped. Cut and finished into lightweight earrings.
  3. Shared. At local markets and online.
