Our story

The story behind Crochet by Sarah Louise

01 How it started

Affordable, unique and modern crochet items

I’ve been sharing my crochet here since 2017, from baby items and hats to the plushies and patterns you’ll find today.

My signature designs are my holiday octopi: Harvest the Pumpkin Octopus, Holly and Jolly, Blu the Blueberry Cheesecake Octopus and more. You can make your own with my patterns, or adopt a ready-to-ship friend.

After a cross-country move from Texas to Washington State, Harvest and I are doing so many new things. Stay tuned!

— Sarah

02 Where & how it’s made

Patterns you can follow, plushies you can hug

Every plushie is crocheted by hand, and every pattern is tested so it’s easy to follow.

  1. Designed. Original characters, from octopi to jellyfish.
  2. Crocheted. Each plushie is made by hand, one stitch at a time.
  3. Shared. Patterns as instant PDF downloads so you can make your own.
