Our story

The story behind Ashe by Rosekate

01 How it started

It started with gifts for friends and family

I’ve been sewing for years, starting with bespoke special-occasion dresses, alterations and skirts in the vibrant print fabrics I’ve always loved.

It was making Christmas and birthday gifts for friends and family that first nudged me to open this shop, and I’ve never looked back.

I add new designs every month, so there’s always something fresh to discover. Do message me if you have any questions, I always reply.

— Rosekate

02 Where & how it’s made

Cut, sewn and checked by me

Many pieces are in Ankara, the beautiful African wax cotton at the heart of what I make, and lots can be made to order.

  1. Chosen. Vibrant Ankara prints and soft chiffons.
  2. Sewn. Every piece is cut and sewn by me in my studio.
  3. Made for you. Sewn to the length, size or print that’s right for you.
