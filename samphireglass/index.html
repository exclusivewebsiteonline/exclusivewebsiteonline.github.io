Our story

The story behind Samphire Glass

01 How it started

From silver clay to a love of glass.

I was hunting for a hobby to try out and decided to have a go at silver clay and copper clay. After about six months of using a flame torch to fire the clays, I bought a small jewellery kiln. That was an investment I will never regret.

It soon dawned on me that fusing glass in the kiln was possible. Soon after buying a glass fusing kit, I realised I have a love for glass. I made pieces for family and friends, until I was told I should start selling them to everyone, and so Chameleon Gifts was born.

Chameleon Gifts just did not quite fit as a shop name, though. Samphire was a name I remember from my childhood, of a plant in the Fens, and so Samphire Glass was born.

— Philip

02 Where & how it’s made

Designed and fired in my studio

“I love the effects, colours and the vast range of shapes I can produce in glass. I am still learning, and hopefully in time I will start to produce larger pieces.”

  1. Fused and dichroic glass. Sparkling dichroic, reactive and patchwork glass, fused in the kiln.
  2. Powder and stencils. Fern leaves, meadows and forests are applied with glass powder through a stencil.
  3. One of a kind. Every piece is individually designed and created, so each one is unique.
