Meet the Artist

Hi, I’m Taylor.

Owner & artist of Taylor Celadon Art

I’m a painter and illustrator who loves exploring different mediums and styles. Much of my work is inspired by character design, video games, landscapes, and so much more.

Our story

The story behind Taylor Celadon Art

01 How it started

A painter and illustrator

I was born and raised right outside of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and I now live in Custer, South Dakota.

I received my BFA in Painting at Arcadia University in May 2020. As a traditional and digital artist, I’m always exploring different styles and themes, so you’re bound to find a piece of art you’ll love.

— Taylor

02 What’s in the shop

Art in lots of forms

From original paintings to little things you can carry around:

  1. Paintings. Original acrylic paintings, like sunflower fields and tiny mushrooms on a tree.
  2. Prints & stickers. Art prints, postcards and vinyl stickers, some of them holographic.
  3. Bookmarks. Whimsical bug bookmarks, plus a bookish little frog.
  4. Pins & keychains. Wooden and acrylic pins and keychains, from flowers to moths.

03 What makes it special

Three things you can count on

Colorful & whimsical

Bright, cute art full of bugs, flowers, fairies and critters.

Traditional and digital

I work in both traditional and digital media, always exploring new styles and themes.
