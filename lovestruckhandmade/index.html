Our story

The story behind Lovestruck Handmade

01 How it started

A lifelong love of sewing.

I’ve been an avid sewist for many, many years, and my background has always been in the textile industry: working in retail fabric stores as a young adult, and later as an accounting clerk for a major Canadian fabric wholesaler for 20 years.

As I no longer sew clothing or costumes for myself or my now adult children, I was looking for something else to keep my sewing passion alive. So I decided to make quality handmade items that I love making, using quality materials.

I’m thrilled that my handmade products have been so well received, with many orders and awesome reviews. It has truly been a rewarding endeavour. Please have a look around, and I hope you find something that you love.

— Sandi

02 Where & how it’s made

Sewn in my home studio in Canada

All items are lovingly handmade by me with care, in a non-smoking and pet-free home sewing studio. Custom orders will be considered; send me a message to ask.

  1. Beautiful fabrics. “I just love creating with beautiful fabrics sew much!”
  2. Reversible cotton. Bucket hats are 100% cotton and reversible, in adult and child sizes.
  3. Ships far and wide. To Canada, the USA and beyond; USA orders are CUSMA compliant, so no extra tariff bills at delivery.
