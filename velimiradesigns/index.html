Our story

Where craftsmanship is a calling

01 How it started

A calling, not just a skill

At Velimira Designs, craftsmanship is more than a skill — it is a calling. Born from the belief that true mastery comes from unwavering purpose, every creation is a testament to patience, precision, and the pursuit of perfection.

Since 2010 we’ve been crafting exquisite leather blank journals, personalized notebooks, planners, books of shadows, spellbooks, diaries, photo albums, and more — each piece designed with care and love.

— Velimira

02 Where & how it’s made

From the first cut to the final stitch

Inspired by artisan traditions and the spirit of luxury, each leather piece is handcrafted with intention and soul. The process is human, deliberate, and personal — because there is no substitute for the touch of the maker.

  1. Choose the leather. Soft lambskin, vegetable-tanned covers, and carefully selected materials.
  2. Shape the book. Hand-cut covers, raised spines, metal hardware, and closures made to last.
  3. Fill the pages. Aged, blank, or ruled heavyweight paper ready for ink and fountain pens.
  4. Make it yours. Custom sizes, locks, titles, and details — message before ordering to collaborate.

What we make

Leather books worth keeping

Blank journals, books of shadows, ring binders, and moon-themed covers — handmade categories from the shop, ready for your words.

  • Books of shadows & grimoires

    Pentacles, triple moons, inverted designs, and statement spell books in soft leather — blank pages ready for rituals, notes, and art.

  • Moon journals

    Lunar goddess covers, moon-phase motifs, and celestial details for keepers of cycles, dreams, and night writing.

  • Ring & screw-post binders

    Refillable 3-ring and post-bound organizers in letter, half-letter, and A5 sizes — elegant frames, gothic styles, and custom covers.

    Classic blank journals and medieval-inspired tomes — from everyday writing companions to large heirloom books.
