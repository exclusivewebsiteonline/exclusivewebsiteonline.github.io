Our story

The story behind 222 Creations

01 How it started

Made to order, never twice the same

Every order is custom made at the time it is placed, so your piece may not look exactly like the picture, and that is part of the charm.

With flame painted copper, I control the pattern and nature decides the colors. It is for adventurous, spontaneous people who enjoy surprises. Enamel is for those who like things a little more predictable.

I hope you find something you like, and I promise to put a lot of love into creating it!

— Maria

02 Where & how it’s made

Fired by torch, colored by flame

Maria fires glass enamel onto copper with a torch, or paints copper with flame so the heat brings out its colors, then finishes each piece by hand.

  1. Cut. Copper shapes, ready for color.
  2. Fired. Torch fired enamel or flame painting.
  3. Made for you. Each order made when you place it.
