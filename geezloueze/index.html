Our story

The story behind Geez Loueze

01 How it started

Started at the kitchen table

I began playing with polymer clay as a way to unwind, but before long I was completely hooked, experimenting with colour, shape, and texture, and discovering just how powerful a great pair of earrings can make you feel.

Geez Loueze was born from that passion. My collections are inspired by confidence and versatility: bold, colourful statement pieces for days when you want to stand out, alongside soft, neutral designs that effortlessly complement any outfit.

I believe jewellery should be fun, expressive, and easy to wear, while still feeling special. Thank you for supporting a small handmade business, it truly means the world to me!

— Louise

02 Where & how it’s made

Lovingly handmade, start to finish

Every piece is lovingly handmade by me, from conditioning the clay to baking, sanding, and assembling the final design. Each design is lightweight, comfortable, and made to be worn again and again.

  1. Conditioned. The clay is mixed and shaped by hand.
  2. Baked & sanded. Cured, then smoothed for a perfect finish.
  3. Assembled. Finished with posts and hoops, ready to wear.
