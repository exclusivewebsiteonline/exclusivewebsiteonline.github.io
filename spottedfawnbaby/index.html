Our story

The story behind Spotted Fawn

01 How it started

It started after having my daughter.

My love of sewing and cloth diapers came after having my daughter. Now, as a mom of two, I enjoy making things for them as well as other people.

My inspirations come from my background as a wildlife biologist and all the beauty in nature.

— Channing

02 Where & how it’s made

Sewn by hand in Utah, one piece at a time

“Hey all! I’m a real live person who sews each item just for your little one. AI can’t sew, yet!”

  1. An idea first. My color block set bounced around in my head for weeks before I finally got time to test it out and see it in person.
  2. Cut & sewn by me. That set took about 3½ hours from cutting to completion.
  3. Find us at markets. Find us monthly at @slcfarmersmarket, @parksillysunday and @bccflea.
