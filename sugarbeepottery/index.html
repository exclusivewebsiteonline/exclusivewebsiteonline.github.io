Our story

The story behind Sugarbee Pottery

01 How it started

One pottery class, and I was hooked.

I took my first pottery class in 2022 and was instantly hooked, and bought my first wheel right after.

I have always loved working with my hands and have been crafting my whole life. When I am not playing with clay, I love knitting, sewing, cooking, and baking.

All the pieces here are one of a kind and handmade with love by my hands in my home.

— Jenn

02 Where & how it’s made

Every step, by one pair of hands

Sugarbee Pottery is a one woman shop where I do all the process to create all the ceramics you see, which includes a variety of steps.

  1. Make. Each piece is thrown and shaped by hand in my home studio.
  2. Paint & glaze. Quilts, checks, sheep and cats, painted by hand.
  3. Photograph & ship. I take the pictures of every item and do the shipping myself.
