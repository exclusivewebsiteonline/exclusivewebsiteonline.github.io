Own your traffic

Your followers, your page, your list

When your Instagram bio links straight to Etsy, those visitors land on Etsy’s page, and you don’t get their contact details. A site of your own changes that.

Bio link → Etsy

  • Followers leave Instagram for a page you don’t own.
  • Etsy doesn’t give you your shoppers’ email addresses.
  • You don’t know who your shoppers are, and have no way to reach them again yourself.
→

Bio link → your site → Etsy

  • Your Instagram traffic lands on a page you own.
  • An email signup turns visitors into a customer list you keep.
  • Know who your customers are: every signup gives you an email address (and a first name, if they share it) that you keep.
  • You can email them directly, whatever happens with Etsy.

What’s on your site
