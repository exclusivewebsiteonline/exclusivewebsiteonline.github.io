Our story

The story behind Alice Carter Ceramics

01 How it started

One of a kind stoneware from Brighton

Alice is a ceramicist based in Brighton, on the south coast of England. She makes one of a kind and small batch functional stoneware — pieces meant to be used, each one a little different from the last.

Her work is inspired by nature. Flowers, stars, leaves, moons and spirals find their way into her designs, sometimes carved through the surface in monochrome sgraffito, sometimes finished in a soft glaze.

Every piece is unique and handmade by her. Thank you for visiting her shop — it means the world to her.

— Alice

02 Where & how it’s made

Handmade in Brighton, one piece at a time

Alice forms each piece herself, then carves or glazes it so no two are quite the same.

  1. Formed. Every piece is handmade by Alice in Brighton.
  2. Decorated. Many designs are carved into the clay — flowers, stars, leaves and landscapes — or finished in glaze.
  3. One of a kind. Small batches and individual pieces, each one unique.

What we make

Mugs, bowls, dishes and more

One of a kind and small batch functional stoneware, handmade in Brighton. Here are a few favorites.

  • Mugs

    Everyday mugs in carved star designs or soft glazes, each one a little different.

  • Bowls

    Shallow bowls and little pinch bowls, carved with flowers and leaves.

  • Dishes

    Trinket, incense and soap dishes — spirals, stars, moths and landscapes.

  • Vases & candle holders

    A soft pink-glaze vase, and candle holders carved with leaves and thorns.
