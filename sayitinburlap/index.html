Our story

The story behind Say It In Burlap

01 How it started

My “me” time

I started Say It In Burlap in 2014. After creating burlap banners and prints for gifts and for my home, people started asking if I would sell my items. I started small, selling to family and friends, and it grew from there.

We are a family of 5, and with 3 kids and a husband who works nonstop, things can be a little crazy. This business is my “me” time: it lets me connect with people outside my circle and be creative, while still spending quality time with my family.

I truly enjoy doing this, and I love focusing on every tiny detail to make sure what you receive is above and beyond what you expected.

— Allison

02 Where & how it’s made

Every detail, painted by hand

Every banner, print and wood piece is designed and hand-painted by Allison, and most can be customized with your own words and colors.

  1. Designed. Your words, season or celebration.
  2. Painted. Hand-painted on burlap and wood.
  3. Shipped. Ready to hang on your mantel or shelf.
