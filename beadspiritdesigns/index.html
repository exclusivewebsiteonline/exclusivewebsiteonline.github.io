Meet the Artist

Hi, I’m Shauna.

Owner & artist of BeadSpirit Designs

Welcome to BeadSpirit Designs! I’m Shauna Zeck, and I’ve been creating handmade, one-of-a-kind jewelry for 30 years, combining gemstones, pearls and other natural materials with time-honored woven beading techniques.

Our story

The story behind BeadSpirit Designs

01 How it started

Rooted in heritage, made by hand.

My jewelry honors my Lakota Sioux, Cree and Delaware heritage, bridging the past with the present in traditional and contemporary designs.

I’m self-taught in seed bead embroidery, including peyote stitch, return stitch and edging. My work has been published in the premiere western magazine Cowboys & Indians, in September 2004 and October 2022.

Most recently, my work was part of an exhibit at the Waterloo Center for the Arts, and it has earned several art show awards.

— Shauna

02 Where & how it’s made

Woven, stitched & finished by hand

Every piece is one of a kind, made in Waterloo, Iowa. Custom orders, like beaded bolo ties, are welcome.

  1. Natural materials. Gemstones like turquoise, jasper and druzy, plus pearls, Czech glass and porcupine quills.
  2. Beaded by hand. Seed bead embroidery and woven techniques like peyote stitch.
  3. One of a kind. Each piece is finished by hand and packaged with care.
