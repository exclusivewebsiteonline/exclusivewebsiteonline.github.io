Our story

The story behind Shared Joy

01 How it started

Where the name came from

The name Shared Joy came to me while holding my daughter Emma as a baby and seeing the expression in a book. I’ll always associate these words with her!

Handmade gifts are the best way to say “I love you,” and this is how we spread joy at our little bow shop: sweet newborn bows, timeless sailor bows, pigtail clips and other accessories for kids.

Thank you for stopping by and shopping small with this mom-run boutique. You can also find my bows in person at local shops and markets.

— Jenn

02 Where & how it’s made
