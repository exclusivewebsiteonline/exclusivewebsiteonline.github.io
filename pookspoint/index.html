Our story

The story behind PooksPoint Beadwork

01 How it started

Raised among the art of Sitka, Alaska.

I grew up in the small town of Sitka, Alaska, where most of my maternal native Alaskan family is from. For 10 years of my life, I was surrounded by tall trees, grizzly bears, ocean, and the captivating art that is created by the locals.

I was lucky enough to be the daughter of one of those artists. As I grew, I watched her sew together tiny intricate earrings that she would sell at local galleries and at different art shows throughout Alaska.

She has passed her skill on to me, and now we work together. Until 2016, these earrings were not available online. My hope is to provide an elegant pair of earrings that will be one of a kind for people to enjoy.

— Chloe

02 Where & how it’s made

Woven by hand, bead by bead

Every pair is hand woven from tiny seed beads, a skill passed from mother to daughter.

  1. Design. Each pair starts as a pattern, like the brick stitch templates I share.
  2. Weave. Tiny seed beads are woven together by hand, row by row.
  3. Fringe. Long beaded fringe is added, then the new pair goes up in the shop.
