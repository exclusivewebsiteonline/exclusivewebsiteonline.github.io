Our story

The story behind Fairyfur Toys

01 How it started

A little story about my work

I graduated with an MA from the Academy of Design and Arts in Kharkiv. During my studies I came across an artist making miniatures from polymer clay, and I really wanted to try it.

My journey into handmade began in February 2018, though it wasn’t my first try at crafts: as a child I sculpted a lot from plasticine and made all kinds of things from whatever I could find.

Two years later I opened my shop. I work in mixed technique with faux fur and polymer clay, and I love experiments and interesting ideas from customers, so write to me!

— Anastasia

02 Where & how it’s made

Faux fur, polymer clay and lots of detail

Every creature is designed, sculpted and photographed by me, and each one is one of a kind.

  1. Sculpted. Mushrooms, faces and tiny details shaped in polymer clay.
  2. Assembled. Combined with faux fur, wire and natural materials.
  3. Shipped worldwide. Packed with care for its new home.
