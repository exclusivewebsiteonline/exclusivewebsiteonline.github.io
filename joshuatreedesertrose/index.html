Our story

The story behind Joshua Tree Desert Rose

01 How it started

A weekend retreat that became home

In 2017 I bought a little house in Joshua Tree with the intention of turning it into a weekend retreat for me and my friends. Little did I know I would fall so deeply in love with the landscape.

The desert is wild, the Joshua Trees nothing short of magnificent, and the eternal skies feel like you can touch the clouds. I am so incredibly inspired.

With my new perspective came new energy, and I met my love. I noticed everything about my surroundings: the smells of the desert, mother nature so close, the wind on my face, the quartz around me.

— Rose

02 Where & how it’s made

Small batches, pure ingredients

I create small-batch body products and candles to enhance your life physically and spiritually. If you’re off to a new start or setting your best intention, I hope you find something you love.

  1. Gathered. Inspired by desert scents like creosote after the rain.
  2. Poured. Candles and balms made by hand in small batches.
  3. Captured. Cyanotypes and photo transfers of the desert she loves.
