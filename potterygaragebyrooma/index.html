Our story

The story behind Pottery Garage by Rooma

01 How it started

Made in a pottery garage

Pottery Garage is my small batch studio. Every piece is either signed by me or stamped with my maker’s mark.

No two items are exactly alike, even if they have the same glaze combination or texture, making each one unique.

I love bold glazes, ocean scenes and textures, and lately some science-themed ornaments are in the works for the scientist friends in your life.

— Rooma

02 Where & how it’s made

Thrown, trimmed and glazed by hand

I use food safe, commercial glazes, and every glaze fire brings a new favourite. Follow along on Instagram to see what is coming soon to the shop.

  1. Made. Wheel-thrown or hand built in small batches.
  2. Glazed. Food safe glazes, layered for drips and depth.
  3. Fired. Each piece one of a kind when it comes out of the kiln.

What we make

Pottery for your table and tree

Mugs, bowls and tumblers, ornaments and jewelry, trays and potter’s tools. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Mugs

    Wheel-thrown mugs in bold drippy glazes and rich textures, from little to extra large.

  • Bowls & tumblers

    Bowls, little tumblers and vases, each glazed one of a kind.

  • Ornaments & jewelry

    Science-themed ornaments, pendants and earrings in glossy glazes.

  • Trays & potter’s tools

    Textured trays, spoon rests and clay texture rollers.
