Our story

The story behind Cotton Lover

01 How it started

Made in Arlington since 2021

Cotton Lover has been sewing soft blankets and accessories by hand, with love, since 2021.

Every season brings new prints, from highland cows and bunnies to sharks, foxes and celestial bears, each one chosen to make a blanket that feels special.

You can find Cotton Lover online and at local markets around Arlington, like the Cherrydale Holiday Market.

— Kimmie

02 Where & how it’s made

Sewn by hand, one blanket at a time

Each blanket is double sided, cut and sewn by hand, and finished with a soft satin edge, perfect for a baby on the way or a cozy throw for yourself.

  1. Prints. Soft flannel, minky and cotton in fun prints.
  2. Sewn. Double sided and stitched by hand.
  3. Trimmed. Finished with a silky satin edge.
