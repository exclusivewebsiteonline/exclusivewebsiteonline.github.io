Our story

The story behind Teddy Bears by Olena

01 How it started

My little handmade world.

My collection includes one-of-a-kind teddy bears, miniature pocket plush animals, tiny dollhouse animals, soft rag dolls with changeable clothes, and personalized felt nursery decor.

I enjoy combining different fabrics, soft textures, and tiny details to create toys full of warmth, personality, and charm.

My biggest inspiration is the joy these little creations bring to children, collectors, and families around the world. Whether you’re looking for a special keepsake, a collectible artist toy, a thoughtful gift, or a unique nursery decoration, I hope you’ll find something that makes you smile. Thank you for visiting my little handmade world!

— Olena

02 Where & how it’s made

Designed, sewn and finished by me

As the only maker behind the shop, I follow every toy from the beginning to the end.

  1. Design. Every bear, bunny and raccoon starts as my own design.
  2. Sew by hand. Mohair, soft plush and felt, sewn by hand in my home studio.
  3. Tiny details. Little clothes, felt houses, suitcases and tin boxes, so each toy is ready for tiny adventures.
