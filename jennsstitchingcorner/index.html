Our story

The story behind Jenn's Stitching Corner

01 How it started

One stitch at a time

I love creating hand-embroidered art because it lets me slow down, be present and turn simple threads into something meaningful.

Every stitch is made by hand, with patience, intention and a little piece of my heart. I’m drawn to the delicate details, textures and imperfections that make each piece truly unique.

For me, embroidery is more than a craft. It’s a way to express creativity, tell stories and create something beautiful one stitch at a time.

— Jennifer

02 Where & how it’s made

Made slowly, by hand

From tiny pendants to big seasonal hoops, each piece is designed and stitched by me, and I share sneak peeks of new pieces on Instagram.

  1. Designed. Seasonal ideas, florals and little stories.
  2. Stitched. Every stitch embroidered by hand.
  3. Finished. Set into pendants, earrings, hoops and beanies.

What we make

Embroidery to wear and display

Pendant necklaces, earrings, hoop art, beanies and patterns. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Embroidered pendant necklaces

    Tiny hand-embroidered flowers, trees and seasonal scenes set in pendants.

  • Embroidered earrings

    Hand-stitched and cross-stitch earrings, light and one of a kind.
