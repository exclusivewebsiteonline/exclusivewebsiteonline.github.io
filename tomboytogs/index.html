Our story

The story behind Tomboy Togs

01 How it started

Whether you’re a Tomboy, a Princess or a Superhero

I was first inspired to sew in non-traditional fabrics by the girls in my son’s preschool. Many of them liked princesses, but a few also loved dinosaurs and Batman, and they still wanted to twirl.

That’s how this started: inspired by my young friends who like to twirl and dance, but want to do it while wearing dinosaurs.

What began as dresses in non-traditional fabrics has turned into so much more. Whether you are a Tomboy, a Princess, a Superhero or a Princess boy, I’ve got something fun for you.

— Emily

02 Where & how it’s made

Cut, stitched and finished by hand

Every pin, badge reel and keychain is designed and sewn by me in felt, and new designs debut at the faire and in the shop.

  1. Designed. Geeky ideas from every fandom.
  2. Sewn. Cut and stitched by hand in felt.
  3. Finished. Made into pins, badge reels, clips and key chains.
