The story behind The Hungry Fox

01 How it started

Color, nature and stories

I am an illustrator and printmaker living in North Augusta, SC. I love drawing quirky, surprising characters and situations.

What inspires me? Color, nature and stories. I love getting really focused, “in the zone,” when it’s easy to create.

A fun fact: I have three boys and I’m a baker. My advice to other makers is to develop good work habits and not wait for inspiration to strike.

— Mikaela

02 Where & how it’s made

Pulled by hand, one color at a time

I paint or draw on translucent paper and use those to “burn” an image onto a screen coated with emulsion. This creates a stencil. Each color is a separate layer, and I scrape ink across the screen onto paper in layers to create the final artwork.

  1. Drawn. Painted or drawn on translucent paper.
  2. Burned. Exposed onto an emulsion screen as a stencil.
  3. Printed. Ink pulled by hand, one layer per color.
