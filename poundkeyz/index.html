Our story

The story behind Poundkeyz

01 How it started

Art has always been my passion

I love experimenting with various mediums, from polymer clay to drawing and designing. Art and creativity have been part of my life since I was young.

I like creating designs that are colorful, playful and cute. I find inspiration in nature, cartoons and anime, and I try to bring those elements into my work as much as possible.

— Curnette

02 The dream

A cozy stationery shop, one day

My dream is to open a cozy and welcoming stationery shop: a place where people can find one-of-a-kind handmade stationery items, like journals, cards and planners.

  1. Polymer clay. Unique handmade creations, from animal charms to little keychains.
  2. Drawing & painting. Vibrant, whimsical artwork, including painted cards.
  3. Designing. Colorful, playful designs inspired by nature, cartoons and anime.

03 What makes it special

Three things I put into every piece

Colorful & playful

Cute little creations with plenty of color and personality.

Inspired by what I love

Nature, cartoons and anime show up in my work as much as possible.

One of a kind

Each piece I create showcases my dedication and love for the arts.
