Our story

The story behind The Snuggle Stitch

01 How it started

Yarn has been with me since I was 16.

I have been crocheting and knitting since I was 16, to help with severe depression and anxiety. After I had my third daughter, I picked it back up again and have been playing with yarn ever since!

Taking the plunge and opening up a shop will hopefully allow me to financially contribute to our family, and I am so excited to see where this journey takes me.

As always, thank you so much for your support and for stopping by! XOXO, Carrie

— Carrie

What we make

Cozy things for little ones and grown-ups

Baby blankets, plushies and snugglers, cup cozies, beanies and Sunday drops. Here is what you will find in the shop.
