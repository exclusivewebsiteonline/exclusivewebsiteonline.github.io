Meet the Artist

Hi, I’m Holly.

Owner & artist of Holly Sellors Art & Design

I am a pet portrait artist, and I handcraft party supplies too, based in Worthing, England. For over a decade I’ve been a dedicated pet portrait artist, but my passion for creativity extends far beyond that.

Our story

The story behind Holly Sellors Art & Design

01 How it started

Two things I love most.

I’ve been creating for over a decade, and my shop is a reflection of two things I love most: capturing the personality of beloved pets in hand-painted portraits, and making celebrations feel truly special with unique, personalised party supplies.

Whether you’re planning a wedding, baby shower, birthday party or a memorial for a much-loved pet, I’d love to help make it memorable. Don’t see your theme? Just ask! I genuinely love a creative challenge.

Thank you for visiting, it means the world. ❤️

— Holly

02 Where & how it’s made

Designed and handmade by me

Every piece of confetti, cake topper, seed favour and garland is designed and handmade by me.

  1. Paint. Pet portraits are hand-painted to capture each pet’s personality.
  2. Design & make. Confetti, cake toppers, favours and garlands, designed and handmade for your theme.
  3. Pack. Recyclable and biodegradable packaging wherever possible.
