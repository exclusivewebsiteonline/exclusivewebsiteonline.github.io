Our story

The story behind My Little Sunflower Seed

01 How it started

A love for vintage quilts

I was born and raised in South Florida. I am a wife, mother and grandmother, and I also have the cutest beagle named Annie.

I have always had a love for vintage quilts, so I decided to create a shop to display my vintage quilt pillows. Each one is made from a vintage quilt and finished with hand-embroidered lettering, so every stitch has that old-fashioned charm I love.

I hope you enjoy them as much as I enjoyed making them!

— Katie

02 Where & how it’s made

Cut, pieced and hand embroidered

Katie pieces each pillow from vintage and antique quilts and finishes it with hand embroidery. Custom orders are welcome too.

  1. Vintage quilts. Antique and vintage quilt pieces, chosen one by one.
  2. Embroidered. Sweet sayings stitched by hand.
  3. Finished. Patchwork pillows ready for your cottage or farmhouse.
