Our story

The story behind Midday Moon Macrame

01 How it started

It started with a plant hanger

Back in 2015, I crafted my very first macrame plant hanger out of necessity, to make space for my ever-expanding collection of house plants. I taught myself along the way.

What began as a few simple plant hangers quickly became a passion for all things fiber art, and after five years of honing my skills I opened my shop in late 2020.

The sight of the moon in broad daylight has always brought me tranquility and comfort. With every piece, I hope to capture that same serenity for your space.

— Whitney

02 Where & how it’s made

Every knot tied by my own hands

Each cord is cut and every knot is tied by hand, with focus on detail and care. My sweet cat Sam often helps.

  1. Inspired. Ideas from hikes and the natural world.
  2. Knotted & woven. Every cord cut and tied by hand.
  3. Made for you. Commissions are open for custom pieces.
