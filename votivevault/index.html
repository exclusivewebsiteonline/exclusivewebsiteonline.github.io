Our story

The story behind Votive Vault

01 How it started

Looking for color, then making it

Twenty years ago I wanted a candle holder that was handmade, with lots of color, on quality glass — and I couldn’t find what I wanted. So I started painting my own.

That search turned into years of hand-painting glass, one piece at a time. No factory, no assembly line. Just paint, light, glass, and a lasting obsession with what color does to a room.

— John

02 Where & how it’s made

Painted directly on glass, on Cape Cod

I’m 70 now and still paint seven days a week, because I still love the moment sunlight or candlelight hits the glass and the room wakes up.

  1. Choose the glass. Quality glass cubes, curves and votives ready for color.
  2. Paint by hand. Mosaic sections, fish, rainbows and seasonal motifs — brushed on one piece at a time.
  3. Catch the light. Finished pieces are made for candlelight, fairy lights or a sunny window.
  4. Ship safely. Breakable glass goes box-in-box, with recycled paper packaging.

03 What makes it special

Three things that matter here

The opposite of beige

These pieces are for people who want their homes to feel joyful, personal and colorful — shelves, windowsills and quiet corners that could use more light.

No factory, no assembly line

Every holder is painted one at a time in a home studio. Mosaic designs take time section by section — and the glow is worth it.

Living color

Beautiful by day; even better lit at night. Candlelight, sunlight or battery fairy lights all wake up the painted glass.

What we make

Colorful glass for shelves and windowsills

Mosaic holders, coastal fish designs, contemporary color and seasonal pieces — hand-painted in small batches on Cape Cod.

  • Mosaic candle holders

    Stained-glass style mosaics in blues, greens, oranges and florals — the best sellers, painted section by section.

  • Coastal & fish

    Ocean-inspired fish designs on curved and square glass — coastal color for tea lights, candles and fairy lights.

  • Contemporary color

    Rainbow circles, pastels, pinks and multi-color modern pieces — bright, joyful glass for everyday shelves.

  • Seasonal

    Fall leaves, Halloween pumpkins, winter snowflakes and holiday gifts — color that shifts with the year.
