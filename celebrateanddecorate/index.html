Our story

The story behind Celebrate and Decorate

01 How it started

Filled to overflowing

I use top quality materials and fill every wreath to overflowing for a full, realistic and timeless look. My wreaths are made to last for many seasons.

My number one tip? Measure before ordering! Make sure your wreath won’t be too large or too small for your spot.

I appreciate every single order and your continued support, and I’m looking forward to lots of fun new designs and making new friends.

— Debbie

What we make

Wreaths for every season

Ornament wreaths, dried fruit wreaths, fall florals and summer blooms. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Heirloom ornament wreaths

    Christmas wreaths overflowing with ornaments in gold, burgundy, silver, blue and more.
