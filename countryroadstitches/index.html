Meet the Artist

Hi, I’m Jenny.

Owner & artist of Country Road Stitches

Our story

The story behind Country Road Stitches

01 How it started

Started on maternity leave

I decided to open this shop when I was on maternity leave with my first son. Now I stay busy between my boys, a full time job and this shop! We also have a little Italian Greyhound in our home.

When I’m not stitching, you’ll find me camping, gardening or out on the lake, sometimes with a cozy wool ear warmer in the making.

Many customers come and visit me for towels every year at Minnesota craft shows and markets, and I’m so grateful for everyone who supports my small business.

— Jenny

02 Where & how it’s made

Crocheted by hand, built to last

Each towel gets a hand-crocheted top with a button loop, so it hangs on your oven door or drawer and stays put. Thick, absorbent towels in fabrics for every season.

  1. Chosen. Thick, absorbent towels in seasonal prints.
  2. Crocheted. A sturdy crochet top, stitched by hand.
  3. Hung. Button it on your oven door and it stays put.
