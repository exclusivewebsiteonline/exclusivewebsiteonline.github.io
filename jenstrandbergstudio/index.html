Our story

The story behind Jen Strandberg Studio

01 How it started

Capturing the magic of Nantucket

From Brant Point Lighthouse to Sconset footpaths and rose-covered cottages, my work celebrates the island and New England places I love.

I love trying new mediums. Paper cutting led to my first tunnel book, a miniature storybook that captures the magic of Nantucket in one tiny piece of art, and to a whole collection of cut paper originals.

Each hydrangea shell is carefully hand-painted and coated with a semi-gloss varnish. They make a perfect decoration or jewelry dish, and beautiful gifts.

— Jen

02 Where & how it’s made

Painted, cut and printed with care

Watercolor, gouache and cut paper originals, turned into prints, notecards, notepads and a yearly calendar. Want your print signed? Just send a message after ordering.

  1. Painted. Watercolor and gouache, inspired by island life.
  2. Cut. Mixed media and cut paper details by hand.
  3. Shared. Prints, stationery and calendars to bring home.

What we make

Art and stationery for New England lovers

Watercolor prints, cut paper originals, hand-painted shells, stationery and calendars. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Nantucket watercolor prints

    Lighthouses, cottages, harbors and footpaths from her Nantucket in Watercolor series.

  • Cut paper originals

    One-of-a-kind gouache and cut paper pieces: strawberries, hydrangeas and lighthouses.
