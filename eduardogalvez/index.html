Our story

The story behind Eduardo Galvez

01 How it started

Zacatecas blood, California bred.

I’m a Mexican American visual artist. I draw portraits and figures in charcoal, make monotypes, and sell my original artwork alongside archival grade handmade paper.

I also make paper by hand and share it in free papermaking workshops with my gold subscription members.

This year I was invited to be part of “Raíces y Sueños”, a group art show at the Santa Barbara Public Library’s McKinnon Gallery.

— Eduardo

02 Where & how it’s made

Paper made by hand

Alongside drawing, I make my own paper by hand from plant fibers.

  1. Palm frond fiber. Hand made sheets from palm frond fiber.
  2. Bamboo mat. 7x10in sheets formed on a bamboo mat.
  3. Mulberry. Textured mulberry paper with a deckled edge, made in Long Beach.
