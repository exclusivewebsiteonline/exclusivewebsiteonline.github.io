Our story

The story behind ktcrawford

01 How it started

Designer by day, artist by night

By day, I’m the creative director in Carlow University’s Marketing and Communications department. By night (and weekend), I create my own line of illustrated goods and art prints.

I like fashion, animals (especially bats, sloths and octopuses), music, hockey and Batman, and all of it finds its way into my work.

The result is quirky, candy-colored goodness: spooky cute enamel pins, stickers, cards and art prints, in the shop since 2009.

— Katie

02 Where & how it’s made

From sketch to sticker

Every design starts as Katie’s own illustration, then becomes a sticker, pin, card or print.

  1. Illustrated. Cryptids, critters and cozy spooky scenes.
  2. Produced. Matte vinyl, glossy glitter, enamel and giclee prints.
  3. Shipped. Packed up and sent out to fellow spooky cute fans.
