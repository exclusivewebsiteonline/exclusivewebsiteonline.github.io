Our story

The story behind Ingrid Quilt Studio

01 How it started

From grandmother’s needles to a Kansas quilting bug

Growing up, I spent countless hours beside my grandmother, learning to knit, crochet, embroider, and sew. She patiently shared her skills, and without either of us knowing it at the time, she was laying the foundation for everything I create today.

Then, in 2010, life took me all the way to Kansas. There I discovered traditional American hand quilting, met my quilting mentor, and promptly caught what quilters call “the quilting bug.” What started as a hobby became a lifelong passion.

My home started filling up with quilts faster than I could use them, while my list of new ideas kept getting longer. Over the years, customers began asking for pillows, table runners, kitchen textiles and personalized gifts, so the shop slowly grew into a collection of handmade home textiles.

— Ingrid

02 Where & how it’s made

Made with my cousin, and a few furry supervisors

Some items are made together with my production partner, who is also my cousin and one of my dearest friends. At home, life is shared with one dog and two cats, who supervise from a safe distance.

  1. Chosen. Beautiful fabrics, often given a second life.
  2. Quilted. Pieced and stitched by hand, from start to finish.
  3. Made yours. Different size or colour? Custom requests are welcome.
