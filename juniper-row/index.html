Our story

The story behind Juniper Row

01 How it started

It started with one lopsided mug.

Ten years ago I signed up for a Tuesday-night pottery class to get away from my laptop, and I walked out with the ugliest mug you’ve ever seen. I still drink my coffee from it every day.

I kept going back: once a week, then every spare evening, then every weekend. In 2019 my husband and I cleared out our garage on Juniper Row, bought a second-hand kiln from a retiring potter, and I opened the online shop. The first order came from a stranger in Oregon. I cried a little.

— Hannah

02 Where & how it’s made

By hand, in a garage studio in Asheville

No factories and no production partners. Everything is made in small batches, about twenty steps from my back door.

  1. Wedge. Local North Carolina stoneware clay is kneaded by hand to push out air bubbles.
  2. Throw. Each piece is shaped on the wheel, so no two are exactly alike.
  3. Trim & glaze. Pieces are trimmed, dried for a week, then dipped in glazes I mix myself.
  4. Fire. Two kiln firings make every piece dishwasher- and microwave-safe.

What we make

Everyday pottery, made in small batches

New pieces come out of the kiln every few weeks and are listed in the shop as they’re ready.

  • Mugs & cups

    Hand-pulled handles that fit your fingers, in sizes from espresso to big Sunday-morning mugs.

  • Bowls & tableware

    Serving bowls, pasta bowls and plates, sturdy enough for every day and pretty enough for guests.

  • Vases

    Bud vases and larger vessels in soft, earthy glazes, for a single stem or a whole bunch.

  • Custom orders

    Matching sets, wedding gifts and pieces for cafés. Send a message through the shop and we’ll plan it together.
