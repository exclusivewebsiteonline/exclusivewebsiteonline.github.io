Our story

The story behind Murayama Woodworks

01 How it started

Practical to nerdy.

Murayama Woodworks is where I make handmade wood accessories, from practical to nerdy: a stand for your headphones, a tray for your dice, a display for a lightsaber hilt, a cutting board for the kitchen.

Every piece is made by hand from hardwoods like maple, walnut, purpleheart and canarywood, and I’ve made custom pieces for customers too.

— Ken

02 Where & how it’s made

Made by hand in California, from real hardwood

Contrasting hardwoods do the decorating: pale maple, dark walnut, deep purple purpleheart and golden canarywood.

  1. Hardwood first. Each piece starts with solid maple, walnut, purpleheart or canarywood.
  2. Made by hand. Made by me in California, one piece at a time.
  3. Made to be used. Dice trays get a soft faux suede floor, and stands are built to hold the real thing.
