Our story

The story behind HoneyBee Curiosity

01 How it started

A love for everything mini.

My overall love for everything mini helped me transition seamlessly from polymer clay jewelry into the miniature world. I focus on 1:12th scale items, and I plan to create a 1:6 line in the near future.

My work has been featured in the American Miniaturist and Dollhouse and Miniature Scene magazines.

I believe in great customer service and delivering quality products. If there’s anything you’d like to see in my shop, let me know! 💛🐝

— Melissa

02 Where & how it’s made

Tiny food, made by hand

Every item is 1:12th scale and each piece is unique. Some sets pair my polymer clay work with 3D printed miniatures from my production partner, Jacked Earth Fine Arts.

  1. Inspired by real life. Groceries, snacks and pantry staples, recreated in tiny detail.
  2. Handcrafted. Made by hand, piece by piece, mostly in polymer clay.
  3. 1:12th scale. Made to stock dollhouse kitchens, pantries and little shops.

What we make

Everything mini for your dollhouse

Groceries and pantry staples, fresh food, breakfast and snacks, and little accessories for every room. Here is a taste of what you will find in the shop.

  • Groceries & pantry

    Tiny flour, chocolate chips, cheese and cottage cheese to stock a dollhouse kitchen.

  • Fresh food

    Lettuce, sweet potatoes and other produce in 1:12th scale.

  • Breakfast, snacks & treats

    Bacon, ice cream, birthday treats and condiments like ketchup.

  • Accessories & décor

    Sandals, letterboard signs and little touches that bring a scene to life.
