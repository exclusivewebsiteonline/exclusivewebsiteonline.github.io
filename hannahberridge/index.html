Our story

The story behind the ceramics

01 How it started

Painted by hand, not transferred

Most ceramics you can buy elsewhere use transfers — mass-produced designs simply applied and fired. Hannah’s work starts with a pencil outline on each blank piece, then is painted by hand with coloured glazes, sometimes in several stages, before its final firing in her kiln.

That produces bright, shiny, permanent colours — and both the designs and each individual piece are unique. No two are ever exactly the same.

— Hannah

02 Where & how it’s made

From blank to gift box, in Suffolk

Blanks are sourced from English makers; Hannah outlines, paints, glazes and kiln-fires every piece in her home studio, then packs each order by hand.

  1. Outline. A pencil sketch goes onto each blank ceramic piece.
  2. Paint. Coloured glazes are brushed on by hand, often in several stages.
  3. Fire. A final kiln firing locks in bright, permanent colour.
  4. Pack. Bubble wrap, tissue, a brown cardboard box, coloured string and a personally designed gift tag.

03 What makes it special

Three things that matter here

Truly hand-painted

Pencil outline, coloured glazes, kiln fire — not mass-produced transfers. Every mug, jug and dish is one of a kind.

Suffolk on every piece

Beach huts, fishing boats from Aldeburgh, and wild flowers from the fields — motifs drawn from the landscape around the studio.

Ready to gift

Each order arrives in a brown cardboard box tied with coloured string and a personally designed gift tag you can write on — or Hannah can add a message for you.

What we make

Hand-painted ceramics for everyday tables

Mugs, jugs, teapots, dishes and coastal pieces — each one outlined, painted and fired in the Suffolk studio.

  • Mugs & cups

    Country mugs, little mugs, cups and saucers — wildflower and berry designs that brighten a morning brew.

  • Jugs & teapots

    Farmhouse jugs, milk jugs and teapots painted with the same countryside motifs — pieces made to be used and admired.

  • Dishes & bowls

    Butter dishes, bowls, plates, egg cups and heart-shaped dishes — small tableware with bright, permanent glaze colour.

  • Coastal & seasonal

    Beach hut and fishing-boat designs from the Suffolk coast, plus candle holders, hanging pieces and Christmas decorations.
