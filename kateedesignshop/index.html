Our story

The story behind Katee Design Shop

01 How it started

A special touch for every celebration

Whether you're celebrating a birthday, wedding, graduation or any other special occasion, my signs add a special touch of sophistication that will leave you and your guests in awe.

I put every bit of love into everything I create, and strive to provide a unique design in each product. Crafted with precision and flair, and customized to match the theme and ambiance of your event, is my specialty.

From elegant scripts to bold graphics and dazzling colors, the possibilities are endless when it comes to creating the perfect sign. I look forward to working with you to make your next celebration come to life!

— Katie

02 Where & how it’s made

Designed and painted by hand

Katie makes many pieces for the Bossier City area and ships all over the United States. Every sign is designed around your names, colors and theme.

  1. Share your idea. Tell Katie about your event, theme and colors.
  2. Designed. She creates a one of a kind design just for you.
  3. Painted & shipped. Your acrylic sign is painted, finished and sent your way.
