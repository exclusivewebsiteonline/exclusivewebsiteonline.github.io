Our story

The story behind The Cozy Rose Handmade

01 How it started

Let’s create together

Every sweater starts as a blank slate knit, and together we turn it into a keepsake piece.

I was so honored to be featured in the TODAY Show’s Small Business Gift Guide. Thank you to all of my customers for shopping small, it really does mean the world.

Custom really means custom: one customer wanted a special sweater for a fall Michigan football game, so I tracked down the perfect navy knit for her.

— Carrie

02 Where & how it’s made

Stitched by hand, one sweater at a time

Choose your knit, your colours and your design, and I’ll embroider it by hand.

  1. Choose. Pick a blank slate knit and your yarn colours.
  2. Design. Names, initials, flowers or something all your own.
  3. Stitch. Every letter is hand-embroidered by Carrie.
