Meet the Artist

Hi, I’m Jessie.

Owner & artist of Amaranth Hollow

Crochet has been my favorite hobby for over 20 years. I am a full time scientist, but I love creating in my spare time. I draw inspiration from nature and folklore in many of my creations, and I am also inspired by so many other fiber artists worldwide!

Our story

Finding inspiration for crochet through nature

01 How it started

It started with a patient babysitter

I can still remember my babysitter being so patient with me as I struggled to figure out the simplest of stitches. Fast forward to the present: I am a full time scientist, but I love creating in my spare time.

I decided to finally open my shop in 2021 after the encouragement of some friends who wanted to support me. After creating an Instagram account to document all the things I made and their processes, I was surprised that more than just a few friends wanted to support me!

— Jessie

02 Where & how it’s made

A bit of darkness in all things cute

While I love all things cute, I like to inject a bit of darkness into my work, drawing on dark forestcore aesthetics, a bit of folklore and the occasional punk influence.

  1. Crocheted by hand. Every creature is crocheted by hand.
  2. From nature and folklore. Birds, bugs, moths, octopuses and spittens (cat spiders!).
  3. Patterns too. Digital PDF patterns, so you can crochet your own.

What we make

Creepy-cute creatures and crochet patterns

Forest themed fiber art, from amigurumi creatures to ornaments and patterns. Here are a few favorites.

  • Amigurumi creatures

    Spittens, rats, opossums and other creatures with a touch of darkness.

  • Birds

    Crochet birds, from cardinals and bluebirds to ducks in winter hats.

  • Bugs & spiders

    Eye spiders, beetles, earwigs and ladybugs.

  • Crochet patterns

    Digital PDF patterns, from a mini sword to a hummingbird clearwing moth.
