Our story

The story behind Doxie Vibe

01 How it started

Earrings that don’t hurt.

For years, I struggled with sensitive ears and could never wear the fun, stylish earrings I loved without pain. That frustration inspired me to start experimenting with medical-grade titanium and surgical stainless steel, creating jewelry that’s safe, hypoallergenic, and actually wearable. What began as a creative outlet has grown into something much bigger.

After losing my corporate job of 16 years, I decided to take a leap and turn this passion into my full-time business.

Thank you for being here — your support means more than you know.

— Lisa

02 Where & how it’s made

Handmade in small batches, in Michigan

No mass production, no shortcuts, just thoughtful designs created to be worn and loved.

  1. Clay. Every pair is handmade in small batches from lightweight polymer clay.
  2. Metals. Posts and hooks are medical-grade titanium or surgical stainless steel, designed for even the most sensitive ears — kids included.
  3. Collections. Designs arrive in seasonal collections, from Autumn and Halloween to Christmas and Prelude to Spring.
  4. Company. My two dachshunds are always nearby, keeping me company in the studio.
