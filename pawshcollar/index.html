Our story

The story behind PawshCollar

01 How it started

It all started with Mars.

PawshCollar started with Mars, our Italian Greyhound. We wanted beautiful collars that were not only elegant, but also comfortable and properly designed for the unique shape of a sighthound’s neck.

When we couldn’t find what we were looking for, we decided to make it ourselves.

Today, PawshCollar creates handmade collars, leashes, sweaters and accessories for Italian Greyhounds, Whippets and other sighthounds around the world.

— The PawshCollar team & Mars ♡

02 Where & how it’s made

Made to order in our small studio

Every piece is made to order in our small studio in Ukraine, using carefully selected materials, including premium Italian leather and quality hardware.

  1. Measure. Each piece is made to your dog’s measurements.
  2. Handcraft. Cut, stitched or knitted by hand in our studio in Kyiv.
  3. Finish. Premium Italian leather, quality hardware and personal touches like engraved names.
