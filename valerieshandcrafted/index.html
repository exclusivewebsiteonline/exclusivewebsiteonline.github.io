Our story

The story behind Valerie's Handcrafted

01 How it started

It started with a pair of earrings.

I’ve always had a love for jewelry, especially earrings. During quarantine in March 2020, I wanted to do my part in helping support small businesses around the USA, so I started searching for unique earrings locally, in Grand Rapids and other cities.

I became fascinated with polymer clay earrings and how lightweight they were. I researched the entire process: which clay is best for jewelry, different techniques and more, and decided this would be a hobby I would enjoy.

Many encouraged me to start selling, so here I am! Enjoy :)

— Valerie

02 Where & how it’s made

Shaped, baked & finished by hand

Everything is handcrafted in my Michigan home, and I’m adding new styles every day.

  1. Designed. New seasonal, everyday and themed styles, from fall pumpkins to school buses.
  2. Handmade in clay. Lightweight polymer clay, cut, shaped and baked by me.
  3. Ready to wear. Finished, carded and packed with care, often with a little note.

What we make

Earrings and little extras for every day

Fall and holiday collections, food and sports lines, teacher styles, kid-size studs, hair accessories, home goods and bookmarks.

  • Everyday dangles

    Lightweight polymer clay dangles in neutral, seasonal and colorful styles, some with agate beads or freshwater pearls.

  • Teacher & occupation styles

    School buses, pencils, apples and TEACH studs, plus earrings for nurses, coffee lovers and more.

  • Youthful & kid-size styles

    Dinosaurs, animals and fun shapes, including kid-size studs with shorter 9.5mm posts for younger kids.

  • Hair accessories, bookmarks & home

    Clay bookmarks with tassels, hair accessories and little home goods like dishwasher magnets.
