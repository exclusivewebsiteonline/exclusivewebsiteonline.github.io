Our story

The story behind Brooklyn Rose Beads

01 How it started

A childhood hobby turned into a business.

For as long as I can remember, I have always been into different craft projects. From creating Christmas ornaments, to crocheting, to card making… I love it all! There’s just something about having the image of a finished project in your mind, then using your resources to make it come to life!

My biggest passion has always been beads. In 2016 I started Brooklyn Rose Beads to turn that passion into a business, and to share with the world the simple joys that beads bring into my life.

— Kendra

02 Where & how it’s made

From a home office in Snohomish, Washington

Brooklyn Rose Beads is based out of my home office in Snohomish, Washington, where every piece is made by hand.

  1. Your idea. A name, a favorite color, a charm or a theme. Custom requests are welcome: send me a message and we can work out the details.
  2. Handmade with love. Each of my pieces is carefully handmade with love in Snohomish, Washington.
  3. Ready for gift giving. Every order ships with a small jewelry pouch, so it’s ready to give.
