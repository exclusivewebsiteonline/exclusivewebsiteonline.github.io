Our story

The story behind SLP made

01 How it started

From Evansville to Pittsburgh

I grew up in Evansville, Indiana, went to college at Ball State University (go Cards), then moved to Washington, DC right after!

I met my husband shortly after, and we’ve lived in Pittsburgh for almost 9 years. We love it here, and it’s the perfect climate for warm winter accessories!

After a successful 2018 season, it was time to open up a shop and gain more amazing customers. I hope my handmade items bring you as much warmth and happiness as they do for me!

— Sarah

02 Where & how it’s made

Knit and crocheted by hand

Every beanie is knit or crocheted by Sarah in soft, chunky yarn and topped with a faux fur pom, including team logo hats, and you can also find her pieces at Pittsburgh markets and shops.

  1. Yarn. Soft, chunky yarns in every color.
  2. Stitched. Knit or crocheted by hand by Sarah.
  3. Pom. Topped with a fluffy faux fur pom pom.
