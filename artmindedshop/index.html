Our story

The story behind Art Minded

01 How it started

The Atlantic Ocean was my playground.

I grew up along the east coast with the Atlantic Ocean as my playground, and my work is coastally inspired with a touch of the countryside.

Some places become part of you. For me, Sebastian Inlet is one of those places. I moved here when I was 11, and in time I started my business here, inspired by the grace of these waters.

What once started as a hobby has grown into my very own honest-to-goodness brand.

— Renee

02 Where & how it’s made

Waves, textures and tranquil colors

Each item is handcrafted with care and inspired by the natural beauty of the ocean: waves, textures and tranquil colors all play a part.

  1. Designed. Coastal ideas with a rustic touch.
  2. Poured. Resin, glitter and color, poured by hand.
  3. Finished. Restocked daily and packed with care.
