Our story

The story behind Snail Fern Studio

01 How it started

Nature, made cozy

Alex is the creative force behind Snail Fern Studio, where she crafts whimsical, handmade ceramic pieces inspired by nature and cozy living.

Since 2020, the shop has been a little world of handmade critters: crochet frogs, mushrooms and worms, ceramic beetle pendants and tiny polymer clay studs.

Closer to home, Alex brings her handcrafted ceramic kitties to Milky & Coco’s Kit-Tea Café in Vancouver, Washington, for painting workshops.

02 Where & how it’s made

Handmade to order, with love

Crochet plush and clay jewelry, each piece made by hand.

  1. Crocheted. Frogs, mushrooms and worms, stitched by hand.
  2. Sculpted. Ceramic and polymer clay jewelry, from beetles to mini studs.
  3. Made to order. Every stuffed animal is made just for you.
