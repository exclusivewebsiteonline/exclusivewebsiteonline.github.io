Our story

The story behind Briar Rose Needlework

01 How it started

A family tradition, stitched slowly

Embroidery runs in my family. It was passed down through generations, and now I get to carry it on.

I stitch slowly, by hand, with designer fabrics, DMC thread and leather. Botanical motifs and vintage color palettes are my favorite things to work with.

I hope each piece is kept, loved and passed down, just like the craft was passed down to me. And for stitchers, my PDF patterns let you make your own.

— Beth

02 Where & how it’s made

Designer fabric, DMC thread and patience

From Library Card hoops for book lovers to custom name pieces for nurseries, Beth designs and stitches each piece by hand, and writes clear patterns for beginners too.

  1. Designed. Original botanical and vintage floral motifs.
  2. Stitched. Slowly, by hand, with DMC thread.
  3. Kept. Made to last and be passed down.
