Our story

The story behind Mochablue Fiber Art

01 How it started

Finding the artist within

I’m a Filipino artist who grew up with fine art all around me, but I didn’t realize the artist within me until the summer of 2020, when I discovered weaving and the fiber arts.

Using fibers as my colour and texture palette, my art is the intersection between subtle and indulgent, colourful and muted.

I’m an architect by day, and find that art is all around me.

— Monica

02 Where & how it’s made

Fibers from small, women-owned businesses

All fibers are sourced from other small and women-owned businesses, in the hope that by using small, we build our community together.

  1. Sourced. High quality fibers from small businesses.
  2. Woven. Each piece woven by hand on the loom.
  3. One of a kind. No two wall hangings are the same.
