Our story

The story behind Lady Bits Co.

01 How it started

Unique, playful and elegant

Every pair of Lady Bits earrings is shaped by hand from polymer clay, then carded and packed in Fort Collins.

Customers say the designs hit a rare balance: unique and playful, but elegant enough for a gala or a cute pair of jeans.

You can find Kay online and at Colorado makers markets all season long.

— Lady Bits Co.

02 Where & how it’s made

Shaped by hand in polymer clay

Kay designs, sculpts and bakes each pair, picking colors and materials carefully so they are light enough to wear all day.

  1. Sculpted. Every shape made by hand.
  2. Baked. Cured polymer clay that stays light.
  3. Carded. Packed on a Lady Bits card.
