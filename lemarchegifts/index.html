Our story

The story behind Le Marché Gifts

01 How it started

From the markets of France to Fort Collins

Whether it's the old book stands along the Seine, the reams of colorful floral fabrics of Provence, the food markets of Champagne and Bordeaux, or the small stands that have popped up for generations in the hilltop towns of the French countryside, my decades-long love of traveling in France has imbued my craft.

So many of my loved ones are equally obsessed with the beauty of France, and it is likely always one of them that inspires me as I curate a one of a kind gift basket, sew an apron or imagine that perfect addition to their table or kitchen.

Gift-giving is certainly a language of love at Le Marché. I hope that, no matter the season, you find that perfect something for those closest to you, or better yet, for yourself!

— Jen

02 Where & how it’s made

Sewn by hand, market by market

Jen handsews her aprons and totes with pockets galore and colorful cotton linings, and brings them to northern Colorado markets like the Wildcraft Market in Fort Collins.

  1. Fabric. Florals, lemons and prints chosen with a French eye.
  2. Handsewn. Aprons and totes, reversible and full of pockets.
  3. Gifted. Curated into gift sets and baskets with love.
