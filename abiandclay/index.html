Meet the Artist

Hi, I’m Abi.

Owner & artist of Abi & Clay

I’m an Irish polymer clay artist from Co. Laois, creating from the heart of the Midlands. From my small studio, I handcraft small-batch collections of my own original designs.

Our story

The story behind Abi & Clay

01 How it started

Inspired by Irish wildflowers.

Floral patterns have always been a big inspiration for me, and they continue to inspire many of the pieces I create.

The beauty of the nature around me here in Ireland, especially the colours and wildflowers that are part of our landscape, has sparked many of my ideas.

My creations range from flowers and colourful clay cane designs to marble textures and patterns inspired by the beautiful palettes we see around us in nature, here in Ireland.

— Abi

02 Where & how it’s made

Made in my small studio in the Midlands

“Autumn palettes have always been my favourites, so these clay earrings are full of warm, soft shades inspired by the change in the season.”

  1. Professional grade polymer clay. Every pair is made by hand from professional grade polymer clay.
  2. Small batches, original designs. Each collection is my own design, made in small batches.
  3. Ready to gift. Every pair comes on an Abi & Clay display card, with a white organza gift bag to store your earrings in.
