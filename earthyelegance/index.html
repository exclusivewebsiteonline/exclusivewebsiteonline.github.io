Our story

The story behind Earthy Elegance

01 How it started

Inspired by nature and simplicity.

Earthy Elegance is a small business inspired by nature and simplicity. Each piece is carefully crafted with a focus on quality and beauty.

Wearing jewelry with plants can offer a unique and meaningful connection to nature, bringing a sense of peace and harmony to your daily life.

You can find my pieces online and locally at Gather Home Boutique.

— Gabby

02 Where & how it’s made

Real flowers, preserved by hand

Every pair holds real pressed ferns and flowers, so no two are exactly alike.

  1. Gathered. Ferns, forget-me-nots, Queen Anne’s lace and wildflowers.
  2. Pressed. Each flower is pressed and set by hand.
  3. Finished. Light, comfortable earrings, packaged with care.

What we make

Bring a tiny garden with you

Fern earrings, forget-me-nots, wildflowers and nature studs. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Fern earrings

    Real fern drops, dangles and studs, from tiny minis to bigger hoops.

  • Forget-me-nots

    Tiny, delicate baby blue forget-me-not flowers set in resin.

  • Wildflowers & Queen Anne’s lace

    Pressed wildflowers and Queen Anne’s lace in little hoops and drops.

  • Studs & seasonal pieces

    Nature studs and cozy fall designs in earthy tones.
