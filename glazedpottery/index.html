Our story

The story behind Glazed Pottery

01 How it started

Inspired by the heart of the kitchen

Innovative, functional and beautiful: these are the words that inspire me most when making all of my handcrafted creations. My ultimate goal is to create unique conversation pieces that encompass both functionality and beauty.

My inspiration originates in the heart of my kitchen, as well as in the infinite beauty found in the world around us.

Something that started from simple dirt can now be used for years to come. I embrace that energy by creating pieces that are both alluring and practical, and I hope my wares bring joy and inspiration to your daily routines.

— Mandy

02 Where & how it’s made

Designed to make life easier

From self-draining sponge holders to berry bowls that double as colanders, every piece solves a small everyday problem and looks beautiful doing it.

  1. Designed. Ideas born in the kitchen.
  2. Thrown. Shaped by hand in Mandy’s studio.
  3. Glazed. Finished in her signature northern-lights glazes.
