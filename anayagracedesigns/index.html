Our story

The story behind Anaya Grace Designs

01 How it started

A dress for the father-daughter dance.

My sewing journey began 9 years ago when I was unemployed and wanted to make my daughter a dress, with a matching bow tie and pocket square for my husband, so they could attend their first father-daughter dance. It started off slow as I re-entered the workforce, but I found myself sewing every chance I got.

In 2018 I found a group of local sewists and started making more items for friends and family. My grandmothers were both master seamstresses from the 1930s through the ’80s, and my mother made holiday decorations over the years. I couldn’t fight the calling any longer.

My daughter is my inspiration, and I love making clothes for her and my son. Named after my daughter and my grandmother, Anaya Grace Designs was born. Building a legacy for my children and finding my happy place is what it’s all about for me.

— Kimberly
