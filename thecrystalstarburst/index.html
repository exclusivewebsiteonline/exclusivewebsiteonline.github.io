Our story

The story behind The Crystal Starburst

01 How it started

A starburst of unique crystal jewelry

Welcome to The Crystal Starburst! My goal is to give you a starburst of unique, high quality crystal jewelry. Every piece is hand created by me, and I pride myself on high quality craftsmanship.

I sell most of my work at local shows and events, but my social media followers usually get first dibs at my creations!

With love, always.

— Mayra

02 Where & how it’s made

Dirty hands, shiny new jewelry

Each stone is chosen for its natural colors and patterns, then wrapped by hand in an intricate design to bring them out.

  1. Chosen. Natural gemstones like jasper, agate, opal and turquoise.
  2. Wrapped. Shaped by hand in solid copper, .925 sterling silver or 14k gold-filled wire.
  3. One of a kind. No two pendants are ever the same.

What we make

Crystal jewelry, wrapped by hand

Copper and sterling pendants, turquoise and opals, and protection bracelets. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Copper wire-wrapped pendants

    One-of-a-kind gemstones wrapped in solid copper: jaspers, agates, opals and more.

  • Sterling silver pendants

    Larimar, kyanite, topaz and turquoise wrapped in solid .925 sterling silver.

  • Turquoise & opals

    Campitos, Royston and Fox turquoise, plus fiery Cantera and Ethiopian opals.

  • Protection bracelets

    Adjustable gemstone bead bracelets, like the Triple Protection bracelet.
