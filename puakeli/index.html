Our story

The story behind Puakeli

01 How it started

“Where can I buy that?”

My journey began in Hilo, Hawaiʻi, where I was working at a Japanese-style bedding shop. Surrounded by the textures and patterns of home, I fell in love all over again with the quiet beauty of Japanese textiles. At the same time, I was learning more about the history of Japanese immigrants here: stories of strength, tradition and resilience that deeply moved me.

At first, making small accessories was just a personal hobby. But everything changed when someone asked, “Where can I buy that?” And just like that, this creative passion became a shop.

Years later, I continue to handcraft each piece from my home in Hilo. I’ve been honored to connect with kind souls of all ages: young women who feel a deep love for Japan, and older women who often share how my pieces remind them of their heritage, travels, or the traditions they grew up with.

— Noriko

02 Where & how it’s made

Handmade at home in Hilo

Every wallet, lanyard and key fob is cut and sewn by me, one piece at a time.

  1. Traditional Japanese fabrics. Cotton prints with sakura, cats, bunnies, camellias and peonies.
  2. Sewn by hand at home. Each piece is handcrafted by me in Hilo.
  3. Custom orders welcome. I also work with customer-provided fabrics.
