Privacy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

What the form collects

When you request a free preview, the form asks for your Etsy shop link and your email address. You can also add your Instagram handle and first name if you like.

Why

We use this only to build your preview site, send it to you, and follow up with you about it. We don’t add you to a newsletter, and we don’t sell or share your details.

Where it goes

Your request is sent to Exclusive Website Online by email, through Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a form-to-email service. Web3Forms handles the message on its way to us under its own privacy policy.

Analytics and cookies

This site doesn’t use analytics, tracking, or cookies. Fonts and images are served from this site.

Deleting your details

To have your request and details deleted, email hello@exclusivewebsite.online and we’ll delete them.
