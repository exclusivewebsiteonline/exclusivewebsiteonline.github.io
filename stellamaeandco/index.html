Our story

The story behind Stella Mae & Co.

01 How it started

Named after Stella, my sweet Golden Retriever mix.

Stella Mae and Co. is a home-based business where I hand make dog products such as bandanas, girl dog bows, bow ties, collars and waste bag holders.

Each product is created from high-quality cotton fabrics and meticulously hand-crafted by me in my home shop in Kannapolis, NC. Everything is made to order.

My company name was inspired by my late Golden Retriever mix, Stella (2010–2026). Stella Mae and Co. is dedicated to providing only the best for your pup!

— Karen

02 Where & how it’s made

Sewn by me, made to order

Message me about matching leashes, or if you see a print you love in a bandana but not in a waste bag holder, bow or bow tie. Chances are I can make it.

  1. Two bandana styles. Tie-on, or collar slip-on.
  2. Fringed flannel. In fall and winter, flannel bandanas with a curved neckline and two sets of snaps, custom made to your pup’s loose neck measurement.
  3. Personalized. Bandanas and collars can be personalized with your pup’s name.
