Our story

The story behind Parsa Leatherworks

01 How it started

Creative ideas, made by hand.

My mission and goal is to fulfill my creative ideas and projects through custom and handmade work.

Every piece, from a simple leather ring to a full set of armor, is cut, tooled and stitched by me. I love working with you on custom orders and bringing your vision to life.

Find me at local markets, renaissance faires and pirate festivals throughout Oregon!

— Parsa

02 Where & how it’s made

Cut, tooled & stitched by hand

Everything is custom and handmade by me, one piece at a time.

  1. Your idea. Send me a message with your idea, colors or size for a custom order.
  2. Tooled by hand. Each piece is cut, stamped and tooled from leather.
  3. Finished with care. Dyed, stitched and finished so it looks great and lasts.

What we make

Leather goods for every adventure

Jewelry, hat bands, wallets, bags, armor and masks. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Leather rings

    Handmade leather rings, tooled or set with a stone, custom made to fit.

  • Dragon necklaces

    Leather dragon pendants with outstretched wings, in multiple colors.

  • Hat bands

    Custom leather hat bands with loops for feathers and trinkets, made to your color and length.

  • Armor, masks & costume pieces

    Hand harnesses, bracers, eye patches and hand-tooled masks for faires, festivals and LARP.
