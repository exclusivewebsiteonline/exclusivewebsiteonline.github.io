Our story

The story behind The Copper Oasis

01 How it started

Something incredible can come from the flames.

I have always been an artistic person but felt I lacked the ability to create the visions in my head. That all changed when, thanks to my father, I discovered flame-painted copper. I quickly became obsessed and have found great joy and satisfaction in this process.

Fire is often a source of destruction, a concept that I am all too familiar with, as my family lost everything in the 2018 California wildfires. However, this artform has helped remind me that something incredible can come from the flames. Copper never heats the same way twice, and there is beauty in the chaos it creates.

I felt the name The Copper Oasis was fitting because, first, I work with all things copper. And second, an oasis is a pleasant or peaceful place in the midst of a difficult or hectic situation. Creating these one-of-a-kind pieces has become a true oasis for me, and I hope you experience a similar joy when shopping with us!

— Natalia

02 Where & how it’s made

All the colour comes from fire

All of the colour and patterning on my pieces comes from applying heat to copper. I don’t use any dyes or chemicals.

  1. Cut the copper. Every piece starts as plain copper, shaped by hand.
  2. Paint with flame. Heat brings out purples, blues, golds and pinks, and copper never heats the same way twice.
  3. Finish by hand. Pieces are engraved, assembled and packaged by Natalia, start to finish.
