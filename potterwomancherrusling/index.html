Our story

The story behind Cher Rusling Tiles

01 How it started

From the lakeshore to the Potters Guild

As a small child I would dig clay by the lake with my hands, make forms and let them dry in the sun. Fast forward to becoming a member of the Village Potters Guild in Plymouth, Michigan, and then in 1999 the Ann Arbor Potters Guild, where I create and fire all my work today.

I have explored many forms and techniques: wheel throwing and functional dinnerware, serving vessels and flower vases, and sculptural vases based on the Anasazi cliff dwellings of the southwest. Then I turned to art tiles inspired by nature and ancient symbols, and hand-built functional pottery.

My work has sold at the Ann Arbor Street Art Fair, Pewabic Pottery in Detroit and the bi-annual Ann Arbor Potters Guild sales. In 2019 I opened my online shop to share my work with a broader audience, and I have been very happy connecting with shoppers there.

— Cher

02 Where & how it’s made

Made at the Ann Arbor Potters Guild

Cher is a member of the non-profit Ann Arbor Potters Guild studio and produces all of her work there. Every tile is individually handmade, glazed and fired.

  1. Pressed. Flowers, leaves and hand stamps leave their marks in the soft clay.
  2. Glazed. Coat after coat of glaze brings every detail to life.
  3. Ready to hang. Each tile is fired and finished, ready for your wall.

What we make

Handmade art tiles for your walls
