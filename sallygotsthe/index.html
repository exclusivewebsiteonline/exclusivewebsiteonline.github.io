Our story

The story behind Jill Emery

01 How it started

Slow, steady growth: my American dream.

Lately, part of my process in painting has been to think about words in a poetic, symbolic, spiritual sense. It’s a two-part process, sometimes in reverse. It’s pretty free flowing and completely cathartic.

As I get older I realize I have grown tremendously. I still feel like the kid I was, and I have a strong feeling for animal rights and kindness in general.

I always feel on the outside, and work my own way with no outside demands. Translation: do what I want, when and how, without bosses, managers, assistants or lawyers. I hope people relate and feel my work.

— Jill

02 Where & how it’s made

Painting as a cathartic process

I stay busy doing different art pieces; it brings me good. I’m thrilled to have repeat clientele.

  1. Words first. Paintings often begin with a word or phrase, taken in a poetic or spiritual sense.
  2. Paint freely. Layered, expressive portraits of musicians, poets, people and animals.
  3. Originals & prints. Original paintings, giclee prints and custom pet portraits.
