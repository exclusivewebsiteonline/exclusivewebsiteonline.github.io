Our story

The story behind Claybraven

01 How it started

The joy of sculpting, cast in stone

Claybraven is the sculpture shop of Braven, a Portland, Oregon sculptor who makes original wall sculptures in weatherproof cast stone: smiling full moons, winking suns, cheeky fruit faces and a tribute to the classic film A Trip to the Moon.

Every piece starts as an original sculpture, is cast in stone and hand painted, then signed. They are made to hang indoors or out, in a kitchen, on a house wall or in the garden.

New characters are always in the works. Lately it's a whole party of mushrooms!

— Braven

02 Where & how it’s made

Sculpted, cast and hand painted

Each design begins as an original sculpture. It is cast in weatherproof stone, hand painted and signed, then carefully shipped, often with a handwritten note.

  1. Sculpted. Every character starts as an original sculpture.
  2. Cast. Made in weatherproof cast stone for indoors or out.
  3. Painted. Hand painted, signed and ready to hang.
