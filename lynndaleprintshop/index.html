Our story

The story behind Lynndale Print Shop

01 How it started

A love for Milwaukee.

I have always had a love for the unique architecture and rich history of the city. From the iconic Milwaukee skyline to historic City Hall, my art prints capture the essence of these landmarks and bring a piece of Milwaukee into your home.

As a Wisconsin native, I have grown to love my home state and the Midwest. I currently live in Cooper Park, one of the neighborhoods that gives Milwaukee its big city feel but small town vibe.

— Josh

02 Where & how it’s made

Original watercolor and ink

“I hope my passion for Milwaukee is evident in each piece.”

  1. Painted by hand. Every piece starts as my original watercolor and ink art.
  2. Printed with care. Printed on high quality paper and ink, so every landmark shows in detail.
  3. Commissions welcome. Feel free to reach out with any questions or commission ideas.
