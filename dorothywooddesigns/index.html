Our story

The story behind Cords & Crystals

01 How it started

A career in craft.

I’ve spent my career making projects for craft books and magazines. In my shop, Cords & Crystals, that experience goes into jewellery, patterns and kits you can make yourself.

My designs bring together cords, crystals, crochet and macramé: bib necklaces knotted with Swarovski crystals, crystal stars, earrings, coin purses and spectacle cases.

Lately I’ve been exploring collage and painting too, with painted vases and printed-paper vessels.

— Dorothy

02 Where & how it’s made

Patterns, kits and pieces made for you

Make it yourself with a pattern or a complete kit, or choose a finished piece.

  1. Patterns. Crochet and macramé patterns, like the bib necklace and the crystal star.
  2. Kits. Kits with Swarovski crystals and the pattern included, from earrings to a coin purse.
  3. Handmade for you. Finished bracelets, earrings and necklaces.
