Our story

The story behind Designs Stained Glass

01 How it started

I am fascinated with stained glass.

I started taking classes 25 years ago, making small suncatchers and night lights. Soon I was creating panels for my family, my friends and my home.

I now have a fully stocked studio in my home, where I spend many hours creating custom orders and new pieces for my online shop and the artisans shop The Hen House in Chepachet, Rhode Island.

I create everything you see by myself. I design many of the patterns, take my own photos, and do the promoting and packaging. And I love the whole process.

— Nancy

02 Where & how it’s made

Designed & handmade in Rhode Island

Stained glass suncatchers and ornaments, framed pressed flowers, personalized sympathy gifts and small panels, each one handmade in my home studio.

  1. Designed. Many of the patterns are my own designs.
  2. Grown & pressed. Pressed flower pieces use flowers I grow, press and dry myself.
  3. Handmade. Every piece is cut, soldered and finished in my home studio.
