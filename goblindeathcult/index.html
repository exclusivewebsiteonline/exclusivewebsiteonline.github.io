Our story

The story behind Goblin Death Cult

01 How it started

Art at the intersection of horror and play.

Goblin Death Cult is led by Jacqueline Tatro, a one-woman circus and mixed-media artist specializing in textiles and block printing. She is best known for her hand-dyed and printed apparel and SIX FEET UNDIES, along with one-of-a-kind upcycled garments and art prints.

Her art is created at the intersection of pain, trauma, horror and play, as a form of healing. She hopes you’ll find both a sense of depth and lightness in her work.

“Fashion goes beyond utility for most people reading this: we use our clothes as a vehicle for self-expression. My goal is to create that opportunity to express for as many people as possible.”

— Jacqueline

02 Where & how it’s made

Carved, inked and printed by hand

Every design starts as my own drawing, carved into a block and printed by hand, often on hand-dyed garments.

  1. Draw. Original artwork: medieval beasts, bats, coffins and horror icons.
  2. Carve. Each design is hand-carved into a printing block.
  3. Print & dye. Printed by hand onto tees, joggers and undies, some hand-dyed or bleached first.
