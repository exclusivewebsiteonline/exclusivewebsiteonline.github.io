Meet the artist

Hi, I’m Silvana.

Owner & designer of Silvana Sagan

I’m originally a city girl from Manhattan who discovered a passion for handmade artisan jewelry and the natural beauty of gemstones during my time in New Mexico. My designs blend minimalist, bohemian, and vintage aesthetics — Southwest charm with urban sophistication from my NYC roots.

Handmade artisan jewelry since 2010. Gift wrap and free USA shipping from Colorado.

Our story

The story behind Silvana Sagan

01 How it started

Manhattan roots, New Mexico spark

In 2010, while living part-time in Silver City, New Mexico, I was taking jewelry classes, learning gemstones and new techniques, and spending days in that historic downtown. The shop grew out of a place I loved, friendships I was making, and simply following my heart — not a carefully mapped business plan.

Sixteen years later I still pour my heart into every creation, and hope you find as much joy in wearing these pieces as I do in making them.

— Silvana

02 Where & how it’s made

From a studio in beautiful Colorado

Each piece is thoughtfully handmade using natural gemstones, pearls, sterling silver, 14k gold-filled, and high-quality plated metals — lightweight, personal, and effortlessly wearable.

  1. Choose the stones. Natural gemstones and pearls selected for color, cut, and how they catch the light.
  2. Build by hand. Gold-filled, sterling, and mixed-metal settings — from dainty everyday pieces to bold statements.
  3. Finish to layer. Designs made to layer beautifully and become meaningful gifts.
  4. Pack with care. Signature packaging, gift wrap, and careful shipping from Colorado.

What we make

Gemstones, pearls, gold & silver

Earrings, necklaces, bracelets, and pearl pieces — handmade in Colorado to layer and gift.

  • Gemstone earrings

    Cascading clusters, dainty teardrops, and hoops in gold or silver — from London blue topaz to turquoise and strawberry quartz.

  • Gemstone necklaces

    Layering pendants, tourmaline and citrine statements, hamsa charms, and mixed-metal pieces designed to wear every day.

  • Pearls

    Baroque and freshwater pearl necklaces and bracelets — elegant, minimalist pieces with soft glow.

  • Bracelets

    Stretch stacks, Bali beads, sunstone and smoky quartz — dainty gold-filled or sterling pieces made to layer.
