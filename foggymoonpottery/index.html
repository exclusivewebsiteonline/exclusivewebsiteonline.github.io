Our story

The story behind Foggy Moon Pottery

01 How it started

Pottery with a little magic in it

Every piece at Foggy Moon is made by hand in my Pacific Northwest studio, from porcelain mugs and cauldrons to butter crocks and jars.

I love glazes you have to see in person: drippy rainbows, black pearl, glacier blue and silvery mirror black.

Keep creating, find joy where you can, and stay hydrated, ideally from a handmade mug.

— Sarah

02 Where & how it’s made

Thrown, glazed and fired by hand

I work mostly in porcelain, with glazes that shine in the light.

  1. Thrown. Shaped by hand in porcelain and stoneware.
  2. Glazed. Layered glazes for drips, swirls and shine.
  3. Fired. Kiln-fired so every piece is ready for daily use.
