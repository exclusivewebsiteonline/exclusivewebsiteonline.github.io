Our story

The story behind Nomadic Novelties

01 How it started

Living in tiny spaces, creating tiny things.

Every piece is sculpted by hand and inspired by the many places I have called home while on the road, and by all the mushroom-related vintage items I’ve thrifted along the way.

I sculpt by hand, with no molds or cutters, with love in each and every curve, and every piece is packaged with the same attentive love and care. All crystals are ethically sourced, and interior packaging is made with recycled materials.

Each piece is uniquely handcrafted, making Nomadic Novelties home to original pieces. I hope you enjoy my work as much as I enjoyed creating it; it means the world to me to know my art makes people happy! Sending love from Oregon.

— Kae

02 Where & how it’s made

Hand sculpted in a bus, no molds

Made-to-order pieces take 1–2 weeks, and I can’t rush them: everything is handmade. Custom pieces take about 3 weeks; message me with your idea and I’d love to bring it to life!

  1. Sculpt. Every curve shaped by hand, with no molds or cutters.
  2. Set the stones. Ethically sourced crystals: agate, malachite, labradorite, quartz.
  3. Pack with care. Sustainable packaging, made with recycled materials.
