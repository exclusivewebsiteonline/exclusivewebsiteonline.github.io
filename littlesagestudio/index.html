Our story

The story behind Little Sage Studio

01 How it started

It began as a quiet weekend escape.

I’m Hiru, the hands, heart, and dreamer behind Little Sage Studio. 🌿 This little shop began as a quiet weekend escape: just me, some clay, and a creative itch I couldn’t ignore.

What started as a simple hobby quickly grew into something much more meaningful: a space where I could turn tiny blocks of clay into wearable charms and cozy moments of everyday life. ✨

I’m so grateful you’ve found your way here. Thank you for supporting this dream of mine. I hope you find something in my collection that feels like a reflection of you. 🌸

— Hiru

02 Where & how it’s made

Handcrafted from polymer clay

Each piece is thoughtfully handcrafted from polymer clay, with inspiration drawn from nature and the soft, celestial kind of magic.

  1. Tiny blocks of clay. Each charm starts as a little block of polymer clay.
  2. Shaped by hand. Sculpted into ghosts, pumpkins, ferns and more.
  3. Small batch. Slow made in small batches, with care and intention.
