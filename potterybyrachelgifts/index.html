Our story

The story behind Pottery by Rachel

01 How it started

From a passion to a small business

I create handcrafted ceramic housewares from my home studio. Each piece, from mugs and bowls to decorative vases, is formed and finished with an eye for both beauty and daily function.

What began as a personal hobby quickly grew as friends and family took notice of my creations. Their encouragement led me to open my shop and share my work with a wider audience.

I also love making pieces with something special in mind: a coffee mug for your grandmother’s 80th birthday or a platter for your best friend’s wedding. You name it and I’ve got you covered!

— Rachel

02 Where & how it’s made

Made by hand, tested with care

Every piece is thrown, glazed and fired in my home studio, and many items mirror color patterns so they go together beautifully.

  1. Formed. Thrown and shaped by hand in clay and porcelain.
  2. Glazed. Finished in vibrant colors, words and art.
  3. Tested. Every piece is filled with boiling water for hours to make sure it’s completely waterproof.

What we make
