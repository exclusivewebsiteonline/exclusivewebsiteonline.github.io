Our story

The story behind Wildwood & Sage

01 How it started

Born from a love of simple, timeless gifts

This shop was born from my love of creating simple and timeless gifts for my family and friends, and I am so excited to extend it to your family now.

Our kiddos get so much stimulation from other areas in their lives. I offer items that help create a calming, peaceful atmosphere and inspire old-fashioned creative play.

Between years of nannying and a couple handfuls of nieces and nephews, I’ve gathered lots of inspiration for collections that spark imagination.

— Rachel

02 Where & how it’s made

Made to order, by one pair of hands

This is a one-woman shop. Handmade takes a little bit longer, and I hope it will be worth the wait.

  1. Made to order. Each keepsake is crocheted just for you.
  2. Soft & simple. Cozy yarns and classic designs.
  3. Shared. Patterns so you can make your own.
