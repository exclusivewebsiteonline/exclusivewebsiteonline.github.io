Our story

The story behind Anomaly Pottery

01 How it started

Pottery made by hand, one batch at a time.

Anomaly Pottery is hand crafted pottery, made in San Diego, California. Every piece is rustic, handmade and thrown on the wheel.

I love working with red, grey and dark clays and finishing them in drippy, earthy glazes: aurora pearl, teal, green and blue.

New batches go up in the shop all the time, and I share the newest pieces (and some fun images, like my ceramic gnomes) on Instagram. Thank you for shopping small!

— Jasmine

02 Where & how it’s made

Thrown, glazed & fired in San Diego

Each piece is one of a kind: the glaze runs a little differently every time.

  1. Thrown. Every piece is shaped by hand on the potter’s wheel.
  2. Glazed. Drippy, layered glazes over red, grey and dark clay.
  3. Fired. Finished and ready for your incense, flowers or kitchen.

What we make

Pottery for your home

Incense burners and holders, bud vases, dishes and kitchenware, planters, mugs and home decor. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Incense burners & holders

    Wheel-thrown stick incense holders and little cauldron cone burners, many in a drippy glaze on red clay.

  • Bud vases & trinket cups

    Small bud vases and trinket cups in green, blue and earthy glazes.

  • Dishes & kitchenware

    Spoon rests, creamers, soap dishes and ring dishes for every day.

  • Home decor & one of a kinds

    Planters, mugs and one-of-a-kind pieces, like a witchy little pumpkin wall hanging.
