Our story

The story behind Awe-Struck Crochet

01 How it started

Learned at grandma’s side

I sat and watched my grandma crochet for years. At the age of 12, I asked her to teach me. The blankets she made were absolutely beautiful, and the colours, stitches and designs made me want to create my own.

Years later, I began Awe-Struck Crochet: a calming outlet in the midst of motherhood chaos with four children. What a dream this journey has been, to have this crochet business and be a stay-at-home mama.

My goal is to give you a little bit of love and joy in every handmade product, while providing an item of heirloom quality that can hopefully be passed down for years to come.

— Allyssa

02 Where & how it’s made

Cherish the simplest joys

Watching your tiniest loved one coo on a handmade blanket, or hearing your children giggle as they read to their snugglers: every piece is made for moments like these.

  1. Made. Each piece crocheted by hand, one at a time.
  2. Personalized. Ask for embroidered eyes or a special colour.
  3. Bundled. Wrapped up with love and joy, ready to gift.
