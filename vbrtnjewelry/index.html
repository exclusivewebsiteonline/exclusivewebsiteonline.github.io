Our story

The story behind VBRTN

01 How it started

Wear nature’s beautiful creations

VBRTN began with a simple idea: feel Earth’s gift of harmony, and wear it. Every piece holds real flowers and botanicals, preserved so they last.

The jewelry is handmade in the Rocky Mountains, with genuine raw stones and gold or silver plated and filled components.

Each order is packed with care, so it arrives ready to wear or to give.

— VBRTN

02 Where & how it’s made

Pressed, preserved and handcrafted

Lilly presses real flowers and sets them into earrings, pendants, phone cases and gifts, finishing each with quality metal components.

  1. Pressed. Real flowers and leaves, dried flat.
  2. Preserved. Set in clear resin so the colors stay.
  3. Finished. Gold or silver components and careful packaging.
