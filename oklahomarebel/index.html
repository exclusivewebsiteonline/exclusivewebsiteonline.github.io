Our story

The story behind Oklahoma Rebel

01 How it started

Handmade leather from southern Oklahoma.

Oklahoma Rebel is a two person shop in Pauls Valley, Oklahoma. Every leather piece we make is cut, tooled and finished by hand.

We take custom orders, from patches and keychains to belts, knife sheaths and tooled shoes, and we love bringing your ideas to life.

We also design our own line of t-shirts, all hand drawn and printed by us!

— Brandon & the Oklahoma Rebel crew

02 Where & how it’s made

Tooled, carved & painted by hand

Custom orders are open, with a 6-8 week turnaround. Send us a message to get yours started.

  1. Your idea. Message us with your brand, initials or design.
  2. Tooled by hand. Each piece is carved and stamped in quality veg tan leather.
  3. Painted & finished. Dyed, painted and stitched to last for years.
