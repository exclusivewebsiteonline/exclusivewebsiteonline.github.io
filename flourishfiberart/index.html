Our story

The story behind Flourish Fiber Art

01 How it started

Just everyday macrame for your life

By day I’m a Special Education teacher, and by night I’m a fiber artist in the heart of Texas. I love macrame and all things breakfast!

This little business started in 2019, and it’s been over five years of putting my little pieces of art in your hands.

I’m shaking off the dust and getting back into the groove, with new pieces in the shop. I can’t wait to make some magic for you!

— Amanda

02 Where & how it’s made

Knotted by hand from recycled cotton rope

Bohemian simplicity in handmade knotted goods and art, crafted from 100% recycled cotton rope.

  1. Chosen. Soft recycled cotton rope in earthy colours.
  2. Knotted. Every piece is knotted by hand.
  3. Finished. Combed, trimmed and packaged with care.
