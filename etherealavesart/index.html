Our story

The story behind EtherealAvesArt

01 How it started

From the Indianapolis Art Center to the torch

The journey started with glass classes at the Indianapolis Art Center, and later a Minor in Glass Art from Ball State University.

Today EtherealAvesArt makes glass about nature: tiny uranium glass birds and opossums, octopus keychains, snails, marbles and earrings. Many pieces glow under UV light, and there is a bird of the day on Instagram.

You can find the glass art in Nashville, Indiana at Spear's Gallery year round, and you can even watch the glassmaking live on Twitch.

— EtherealAvesArt

02 Where & how it’s made

Made in the flame, live

Each piece is shaped by hand from borosilicate and uranium glass at the torch, often live on Twitch, then packed safely for shipping.

  1. Melted. Borosilicate and uranium glass shaped in the flame.
  2. Sculpted. Birds, opossums and snails formed by hand.
  3. Glowing. Uranium glass pieces glow under UV light.
