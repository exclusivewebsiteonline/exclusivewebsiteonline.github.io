Our story

The story behind Late Night Loops

01 How it started

Stay caffeinated and comfy

Late Night Loops is all about nighttime caffeine and crocheted creations. Every plushie, doll and accessory is crocheted by hand, one stitch at a time.

You can often find me at Cincinnati-area craft shows and markets, from Cov Craft in Covington to the Cincinnati Comic Expo, meeting so many amazing artists and fantastic humans who also love fiber arts.

Have an idea? Feel free to message me if you have any questions or would like to discuss a custom order. Stay caffeinated and comfy!

— Jessica

02 Where & how it’s made

Crocheted by hand, with plenty of coffee

Soft, chunky yarns, sparkly eyes and lots of personality. Many pieces are one of a kind, and custom requests are always welcome.

  1. Imagined. A new friend, or your custom idea.
  2. Crocheted. Hand-stitched with soft, cuddly yarn.
  3. Adopted. Shipped quickly to its new home.
