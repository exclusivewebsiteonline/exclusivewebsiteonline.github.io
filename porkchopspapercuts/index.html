Our story

The story behind PorkChops Paper Cuts

01 How it started

How it all began

I discovered papercutting by chance in 2013 while flicking through some art and craft books from the library. A friend was getting married, and my partner suggested I make something. I hadn't done anything artistic since school, but as soon as I picked up a beginner's guide to paper craft I had a feeling I could do it.

I started to design my own papercuts and picked up commissions from friends, then friends of friends, then complete strangers, and PorkChops continued to grow from there.

Papercutting gives me mindful moments of peace and calm. I love the challenge of a new commission with a skeleton of an idea, and I've managed some tough ones, like 'Goats - in Outer Space - with McFly song Lyrics'!

— Ben

02 Where & how it’s made

Cut by hand, with the planet in mind

Everything is made by hand, from personalised cards to large papercut commissions. Ben uses recycled paper from a sustainable source and eco friendly inks, plant based biodegradable sleeves and no unnecessary plastic.

  1. Sketched. Every design starts as an idea, an image or a line of a song.
  2. Cut. Cut by hand from recycled paper, or into real leaves.
  3. Sent. Plastic-free packaging and a free recycled kraft postcard.
