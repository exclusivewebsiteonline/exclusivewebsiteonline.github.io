Our story

The Honey & Velvet story

01 How it started

Many sketchbooks later.

Drawing has always been a big part of my life. After years of practice, and many sketchbooks later, I decided it was time to make my own space for all that hard work!

Here you will find illustrations of all your favourite four-legged friends, and a little something to send to loved ones which reminds them of their beloved pets.

I also draw custom pet portraits, so message me to enquire about yours. Birthday portraits are my favourite to create!

— Jess

02 Where & how it’s made

Hand designed in the Cotswolds

Every card, print and portrait is hand designed by me, one much-loved breed at a time.

  1. Hand designed. All my items are designed by me, based on the things I love.
  2. So many breeds. Cockapoos, cavapoos, cocker spaniels, pomeranians, dachshunds, whippets and more.
  3. Custom pawtraits. Send me a message to enquire about a hand-drawn portrait of your own pet.
