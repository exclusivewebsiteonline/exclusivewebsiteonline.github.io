Our story

The story behind LivFree Art and Designs

01 How it started

Two decades of paint and adornment

Painting and jewelry making have been part of my life for twenty years. My fine art prints are reproductions of my original paintings, from giraffes and tree frogs to mystic horses and tropical botanicals.

The LivFree holsters began in my California home studio, when I wanted a beautiful way to carry a vape pen.

As they grew, I expanded into a small fair trade collection made together with leather and metal artisans in Bali.

— Erica

02 Where & how it’s made

From the studio to a small fair trade collection

Erica paints the originals behind every print and designs each holster and pendant, working with artisans in Bali on the leather and metal collection.

  1. Painted. Original animal and botanical art.
  2. Designed. Holsters and pendants by Erica.
  3. Crafted. Fair trade leather and metal work.
