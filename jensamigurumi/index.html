Our story

The story behind Jen’s Amigurumi

01 How it started

One kit, and I was hooked.

I started crocheting in 2022 after picking up a Woobles kit that taught me the basics, and I’ve been hooked ever since! 🧶

I opened my shop, Jen’s Amigurumi, that same year, where I share plushies inspired by my favorite video games, anime, and other happy things. I hope they bring you as much joy as they bring me!

Thank you so much for being here and supporting my amigurumi business. 💕

— Jen

02 Where & how it’s made

Crocheted by hand, stitch by stitch

All items and patterns are 100% handmade by me.

  1. Design. Each plushie starts as an idea from a game, an anime or something happy.
  2. Crochet. Stitched by hand into a soft, cozy plushie.
  3. Share. Many designs become easy-to-follow PDF patterns, so you can make your own.
