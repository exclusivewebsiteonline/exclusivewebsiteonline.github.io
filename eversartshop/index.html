Our story

The story behind Evers Art Shop

01 How it started

Falling in love with the pottery process

When taking a random pottery class at Cuesta College in San Luis Obispo, CA, I knew I had fallen in love with the process. I was getting my welding degree at the time and had a weird obsession with extreme heat. I ended up getting my degree but then figuring out how to continue my life with ceramics.

The fact that you can bring something into existence by using clay totally intrigued me. Of course, the thousands of degrees Fahrenheit needed to fire the work was also super exciting. Then again, maybe watching liquid glaze turn to glass was my favorite part. Who am I kidding? I love the entire process!

— Jazmin

02 Where & how it’s made

By hand, in Long Beach, California

Every piece is handmade by Jazmin — wheel-thrown, glazed, and fired with the same care that first pulled her away from welding and into clay.

  1. Throw. Clay is shaped on the wheel into mugs, bowls, vases, dishes and more.
  2. Trim & dry. Pieces are refined by hand and dried before the first firing.
  3. Glaze. Liquid glaze is painted on — then the kiln turns it into glass.
  4. Fire. High-heat firings finish each one-of-a-kind piece for everyday use.

What we make

Wheel-thrown pottery for everyday joy

New pieces land in the shop as they come out of the kiln — mugs, bowls, vases, pet pottery and seasonal collections.

  • Mugs & cups

    Wheel-thrown coffee and tea mugs with thoughtfully pulled handles — everyday pieces meant to be used and loved.

  • Bowls & dishes

    Serving bowls, shallow dishes and tableware with rich glazes and textures for the table and the kitchen.

  • Vases & plant pots

    From bud vases and mommy-and-mini magnet pots to larger vessels — pottery that brings life into a room.

  • Pet pottery

    Boston Terrier ornaments, custom pet portraits and other clay tributes for the animals who share your home.
