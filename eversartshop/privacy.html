Privacy

Your privacy, in plain words

This site is run by Jazmin Evers, the owner of Evers Art Shop. Here’s exactly what happens with your information.

What we collect

Only what you type into the signup form: your email address and, if you choose to share it, your first name, plus the date you signed up.

Why

To send you your discount code and, now and then, news, new pieces and offers from Evers Art Shop. Nothing else.

Where it’s kept

Your details are stored privately on this website’s server. They are never sold, rented or shared. The site is built and hosted by Exclusive Website Online on Jazmin Evers’s behalf.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time, or ask for your details to be deleted, by emailing hello@eversartshop.exclusivewebsite.online.

Cookies and tracking

No tracking or advertising cookies, and no analytics. Your browser keeps one small note (local storage) so the signup popup doesn’t reappear after you’ve signed up or said no thanks; it expires after 30 days. Fonts and images load from this site only. Purchases happen on Etsy, under Etsy’s privacy policy.
