Our story

The story behind Jewelry by Michelle

01 How it started

Simple, dainty, made for every day

Quality is my hallmark. I carefully source materials and hand-select every gemstone and pearl to craft jewelry that’s enduring and accessible.

I use hypoallergenic, skin-safe materials like 14k gold filled and 925 sterling silver, with real pearls and gemstones in lightweight designs that fit your everyday life.

I make pieces for brides, women and girls, and meaningful gifts like bridal pearl earrings and gemstone necklaces. Thank you for being part of my dream’s growth! XOXO

— Michelle

02 Where & how it’s made

Made bead by bead

Even the smallest details matter: every choker is made bead by bead, and I handcraft my own French ear wires from gold-filled wire.

  1. Selected. Genuine gemstones and freshwater pearls, chosen by hand.
  2. Crafted. Strung and finished in 14k gold fill or sterling silver.
  3. Layered. Dainty pieces made to mix, match and last.

What we make
