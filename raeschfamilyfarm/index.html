Our story

The story behind Raesch Family Farm

01 How it started

From the big city to a little farm.

My family and I moved from a large city that we had lived in for 25 years to a very small one in Southern Oregon. We had 3 children ages 7, 5 and 2, and one on the way!

We bought a property with a little acreage for our children to have room to run and play. That allowed us to add some animals to our already-growing family too. Here came the goats, chickens, dogs, cat, ducks, and more. We had a lot of fun busy going on!

In time we could see the change in climate was affecting our skin, especially our children’s. We had our herd of milk goats, did some research, and started formulating better skin products for our family: healthier skin in such a dry climate, made with more natural ingredients, using sources that were around us.

— Brooke

02 Where & how it’s made

Made on the farm, in small batches

Our soaps are made with more natural ingredients, using sources that are around us, starting with milk from our own goats.

  1. Goat milk. From our own herd of milk goats.
  2. Small batches. Handcrafted farm soap, in scents from Lavender/Charcoal to Lemon Curd, or unscented.
  3. Local hands. Our soap savers are crocheted and hand knitted by Novalee in Klamath Falls.
