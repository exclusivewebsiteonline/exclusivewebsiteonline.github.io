Our story

The story behind Made by Lucy D

01 How it started

It began with learning the basics

A few years back, I began to learn the basics of jewellery making, and things have gone from there.

I make charm jewellery because I have learnt that it makes people smile. I love helping people to get the right piece of jewellery to match their outfit and make them feel and look good.

It’s the perfect shop for your science earring needs, with many other earrings, necklaces and hair accessories too.

— Lucy

02 Where & how it’s made

Beaded and finished by hand

Each piece is made by hand, with silver plated or sterling silver hooks and clip-on options for earrings. Every order is sent with care, often with a handwritten note.

  1. Chosen. Beads, crystals and charms with personality.
  2. Made. Beaded and assembled by hand.
  3. Sent. Packed with care and a handwritten note.

What we make

Jewellery with a little personality

Science earrings, charm earrings, beaded necklaces and hair accessories. Here is what you will find in the shop.

  • Science earrings

    DNA, atoms, neurons, microscopes and molecules for science lovers, students and teachers.

  • Charm earrings

    Bees, bats, dragons, music notes and more fun charms that make people smile.

  • Beaded necklaces

    Hematite star, smiley face, seed bead and glow-in-the-dark beaded necklaces.

  • Headbands & hair

    Crystal headbands, hair vines and accessories, plus memory wire bracelets.
