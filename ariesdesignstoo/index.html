Our story

The story behind Aries Designs Too

01 How it started

A lifetime love of color, flowers and dangly earrings

The artist behind Aries Designs Too has had a passion for long, colorful dangly earrings since she was a teenager. Her friends always said they wouldn’t know her without them, so designing jewelry was the natural next step.

She went into the floral business in 1982, learned from a master floral designer, and later added jewelry design to her training. In 1992 she opened Aries Designs and Gifts, selling floral work alongside her own handcrafted jewelry. After a move in 1998 she opened a new shop called Aries Designs Too, and she has gone on to sell her designs in several local shops as well.

The shop is dedicated to her daughter Samantha, who loved fashion, design and pretty things, and who wore the earrings made for her.

02 Where & how it’s made

One-of-a-kind jewelry, handmade in Pennsylvania

A designer and teacher for decades in both jewelry and floral design, now making original pieces at home in Pennsylvania.

  1. Designed. Colorful, one-of-a-kind combinations, from daytime wear to nighttime glam.
  2. Gathered. Artisan lampwork beads from artists around the world, together with polymer clay, enamel, crystal and pearls.
  3. Finished. Every order comes gift boxed. Custom creations are welcome, and she is a member of the Self-Representing Artist Jewelry Designers (SRAJD).
